Null uses a compact vocabulary, and most of it earns its place. A few words, such as Receiver and Payer, name ideas you will not meet elsewhere, while others, such as Cash, mean something more specific here than they do on an exchange. The definitions below follow the app.

**AER**
The annual equivalent rate is the yearly figure implied by the current Savings supply rate, shown to two decimal places. It moves as the rate moves, so it describes today rather than promising a year.

**Burn**
Burning is how the app describes redeeming a pool or vault share. It is the exit half of the ERC-4626 flow, and it returns the underlying asset.

**Buy and Sell**
**Buy** and **Sell** are the Spot actions for opening or adding to a pool position and for leaving one. Onchain they are ERC-4626 deposit and redeem, whatever the button says.

**Carry**
Carry is the net of collateral yield and funding on a position after the pool ratio has had its say. It is shown on Margin positions and should not be confused with the Morpho borrow APR.

**Cash**
Cash is the USDC sitting in your connected wallet, available to spend. It is not held by Null, and it earns nothing until you deploy it.

**Collateral APR**
Collateral APR is the annualized yield the market's collateral vault is currently earning. It is the raw material from which funding is paid.

**Collateral vault**
The collateral vault is the ERC-4626 share, `nmUSDC`, that a market uses as its collateral. The yield it generates is the source of all funding.

**Coverage**
Coverage is `P / R`, Payer assets divided by Receiver assets. It tells you how thickly the backing pool stands behind the exposed one.

**Funding APR**
Funding APR is the annualized collateral yield paid from the Receiver pool to Payers. It is funded by real yield, and it does not go negative.

**LLTV**
The LLTV is a Morpho book's liquidation loan to value threshold. A Margin position that reaches it can be liquidated by the book.

**Long book**
A long book is a Morpho market that lends USDC against Receiver shares. It is the borrowing venue behind leveraged long exposure.

**Margin**
Margin is the product that borrows on Morpho to hold an enlarged pool position. It can be liquidated, which is what separates it from Spot.

**Mint**
Minting is the app's word for depositing into a pool or vault and receiving shares. It is the entry half of the ERC-4626 flow.

**Net APR**
Net APR combines collateral yield with funding for the side you hold, with Payer figures scaled by `R / P`. It is a current reading, not a forecast of profit.

**nmUSDC**
`nmUSDC` is the Morpho Vault V2 share used as Null collateral. Its underlying asset is USDC, and its yield funds the whole design.

**Null Savings Vault**
The Null Savings Vault is that same `nmUSDC` vault reached through Savings, where depositing USDC lets it allocate across long books.

**Payer**
Payer is the pool that backs Receiver, structured as a negative size position in the Receiver pool with the market's collateral inside it. It gains when the oracle price falls and collects funding for the risk.

**Pro Mode**
Pro Mode is the account setting that reveals **Payer buy** and **Payer sell** on Spot. Leaving it off keeps the ticket Receiver only.

**R and P**
`R` and `P` are the assets held by the Receiver and Payer pools, both counted in collateral units. Nearly every ratio in Null derives from these two numbers.

**Receive**
**Receive** shows your wallet address and a QR code so assets can be sent to you. It neither buys a position nor moves any funds itself.

**Receiver**
Receiver is the pool that takes the oracle tracked return, rising and falling with the price it follows. Its collateral yield is paid away to Payers as funding.

**Settlement**
Settlement is the market update that refreshes a market before pool actions run. It is part of those actions rather than a separate step you perform yourself.

**Short book**
A short book is a Morpho market that lends Receiver shares against `nmUSDC`. It supplies the borrowing behind leveraged short exposure.

**Short scale**
Short scale is `R / P`, the size of Payer's negative Receiver exposure relative to Payer capital. It moves whenever either pool grows or shrinks, so hedges sized from it need revisiting.
