A few words, such as Receiver and Payer, name ideas you won't meet elsewhere. Others, such as Cash, mean something more specific here than on an exchange. These definitions follow the app.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **AER** | The Savings rate, as a yearly figure to two decimal places. It describes today, not a promise for the year. |
| **Back markets** | Holding Payer: taking the other side of a market for both pools' interest. See [Payers](/payers). |
| **Buy and Sell** | The Spot actions for entering and leaving a pool. Onchain they are ERC-4626 deposit and redeem. |
| **Burn and Mint** | The app's words for redeeming and depositing a pool or vault share. |
| **Carry** | What a Margin position earns minus what it pays, per year: USDCn interest on what you hold, less borrow cost and USDCn interest on what you owe. |
| **Cash** | The USDC in your connected wallet. Null doesn't hold it, and it earns nothing until you use it. |
| **Collateral APR** | What the Null Collateral Vault is earning now. The source of all funding. |
| **Coverage** | `P / R`: how thickly Payers back Receivers. |
| **Funding** | The interest on Receiver deposits, paid to Payers. It only flows one way and is never negative. |
| **Funding APR** | Funding as a yearly rate, measured on the Receiver pool. |
| **Leverage Long** | Borrowing USDCn on a long market to hold more of a synthetic. See [Margin](/products/margin). |
| **Leverage Short** | Borrowing a synthetic on a short market and selling it. See [Margin](/products/margin). |
| **LLTV** | A Morpho market's liquidation loan-to-value; 96.5% on Null markets. A Margin position that reaches it can be liquidated. |
| **Long market** | The Morpho market that lends USDCn against a synthetic, written `AAPLr \| USDCn`. The Null Savings Vault lends into it. |
| **Net APR** | Interest and funding combined for the side you hold. Payer figures are scaled by `R / P`. A current reading, not a forecast. |
| **Null Collateral Vault** | The curated vault of USDC that backs every market. Its interest pays the Payers. |
| **Null Savings Vault** | A Morpho vault that holds USDCn and lends it into every long market. What you use through Savings. Its share is USDCns. |
| **Payer** | The pool that backs Receivers. It gains when the price falls and earns both pools' interest. |
| **Pool ratio cap** | A Receiver buy or a Payer sell cannot leave the Receiver pool more than five times the Payer pool. It keeps enough Payer capital behind Receivers to cover a 20% rise. See [Pool ratio cap](/protocol/pool-ratio-cap). |
| **Pro Mode** | The account setting that shows **Payer buy** and **Payer sell** on Spot. |
| **R and P** | The assets in the Receiver and Payer pools, in USDCn. Most ratios in Null come from these two numbers. |
| **Receive** | Shows your wallet address and a QR code. It doesn't trade or move funds. |
| **Receiver** | The pool that follows the price. Its deposits' interest goes to Payers. |
| **Receiver Pool share** | The token you hold as a Receiver. A synthetic, such as AAPLr. |
| **Settlement** | The update a market runs before any deposit or withdrawal: price moves and funding, then your trade. |
| **Short market** | The Morpho market that lends a synthetic against USDCn, written `USDCn \| AAPLr`. Holders lend into it. |
| **Short scale** | `R / P`: how much short exposure each Payer dollar carries. It moves as the pools change. |
| **Synthetic** | A Receiver Pool share named after the asset it follows: AAPLr, SPXr, BTCr, GOLDr. |
| **USDCn** | The share of the Null Collateral Vault. It is the collateral in every market. |
| **USDCns** | The share of the Null Savings Vault. |
