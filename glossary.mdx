**AER**
Annual equivalent rate. On Savings, what you would earn in a year at the current vault supply rate. It changes. The app prints two decimal places.

**Buy / Sell**
Spot actions. Buy opens or adds a pool position. Sell exits it. Onchain these are ERC-4626 deposit and redeem.

**Carry**
Net of collateral yield and funding on a position, after the pool ratio. Shown on Margin positions. Not the same as Morpho borrow APR.

**Cash**
USDC in your connected wallet.

**Collateral APR**
Annualized yield on the market's collateral vault.

**Collateral vault**
The ERC-4626 share a market uses as collateral (`nmUSDC`). Its yield is the source of funding.

**Coverage**
`P / R`. Payer assets divided by Receiver assets.

**Funding APR**
Annualized collateral yield paid from the Receiver pool to Payers. It does not go negative.

**LLTV**
Liquidation loan-to-value on a Morpho book. A Margin position can be liquidated at this level.

**Long book**
Morpho market that lends USDC against Receiver shares.

**Net APR**
Collateral yield combined with funding for the side you hold.

**nmUSDC**
The Morpho Vault V2 share used as Null collateral. Underlying asset is USDC.

**Null Savings Vault**
The same vault, when you deposit USDC through Savings. It allocates to long books.

**Payer**
The pool that backs Receiver. Economically a negative-size position in the Receiver pool, with the market's collateral inside it. Short scale is `R / P`.

**Pro Mode**
Account setting that shows Payer buy and sell on Spot.

**R and P**
Receiver pool assets and Payer pool assets, in collateral units.

**Receive**
Shows your wallet address so someone can send you assets. It does not open a position.

**Receiver**
The pool that takes the oracle-tracked return.

**Short book**
Morpho market that lends Receiver shares against `nmUSDC`.

**Short scale**
`R / P`. How large the negative Receiver exposure is, relative to Payer capital.
