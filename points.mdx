Open `/points`. The page says **Season 0 has started**.

Normal trading activity is being monitored. That activity counts toward points on the page. The detailed rewards view may expand later. `/referrals` redirects to `/points`.

## What points are not

Points do not increase the value of a pool share. They do not change settlement, funding, or a Morpho position.

Points are not money, interest, a fee rebate, or a claim on Null. They are not a promise of a token, an allocation, or a payout. Null can change or stop points at any time.
