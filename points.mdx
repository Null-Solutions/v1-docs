Season 0 has started. You can follow it at `/points`, and the older
`/referrals` address now redirects there, so an old bookmark will still
land in the right place.

The idea for this season is deliberately plain. Normal trading activity
on Null is being monitored, and that activity counts toward the points
shown on the page. You do not need to learn a separate game or route your
trading through unfamiliar products to take part. Use Null as you would
have used it anyway, and the page keeps score in the background. The
detailed rewards view may expand later, but nothing about the current
season depends on features that have not shipped yet.

## What points are, and what they are not

It is worth being exact here, because points programs invite assumptions.
Points are a record of activity, and only that. They do not increase the
value of a pool share, and they play no part in settlement, funding, or a
Morpho position. A position earns exactly what its product would pay if
points did not exist.

Points are also not money. They are not interest, not a yield, and not a
fee rebate, and they are not a claim against Null. Above all, they are not
a promise. Nothing on the points page commits Null to a token, an
allocation, a conversion, or a payout of any kind, at any date. Null can
change how points are counted, change who is eligible, or bring the
program to an end at any time.

That framing should guide how you trade. A position worth taking on its
own economics is worth taking. A position you would not otherwise want
does not become attractive because it is being monitored, since the thing
being accumulated has no stated value. Trade the products, let Season 0
count what it counts, and read the [Legal notices](/legal) for the full
terms that govern the program.
