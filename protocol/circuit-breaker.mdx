---
title: "Circuit breaker"
sidebarTitle: "Circuit breaker"
description: "A single settlement that moves the oracle price by more than 20% skips price transfer and resets the baseline."
---

`MAX_ABS_CHANGE_BPS` is **2000**. `MAX_BASIS_POINTS` is **10000**. One settlement may apply at most a 20% price move.

```text
abs(newPrice - storedPrice) > storedPrice * 2000 / 10000
```

When that is true, `finalizeUpdate` sets `circuitBreakerFired` and skips the price transfer. It still writes `newPrice` into storage, so the next settlement measures from the new price. Skipped movement is not paid later.

Funding still runs on that settlement, subject to the pool floor and the funding model.

The check is not scaled by elapsed time. A 20% move over a minute and a 20% move over a day are treated the same. Two accepted moves of 19% settle in full, one after the other. Size for a path of accepted moves, not only for the single-tick cap.

A zero oracle price reverts before this check. If `oracle.price()` itself reverts, settlement reverts and the market does not update.

`UpdateState` includes the boolean so indexers can show that a tick was skipped.
