Every dollar that enters Null goes into the Null Collateral Vault first. You deposit USDC and the vault gives you nUSDC, its share. nUSDC is the collateral in every market, so it is the one currency that the rest of Null is built on.

## What it is

| | |
| --- | --- |
| The vault | A curated Morpho Vault V2 over USDC. |
| Its share | nUSDC. |
| What it earns | The vault lends USDC out in DeFi. Interest shows up as each nUSDC becoming worth more USDC over time, not as new tokens arriving. |
| Where it's used | Every market holds nUSDC in its pools. The long and short markets lend it and take it as collateral. The Savings Vault holds it. |

You rarely touch nUSDC directly. When you **Buy** on Spot or open a Margin trade, your USDC becomes nUSDC on the way in. When you leave, it comes back as USDC.

## Where the USDC goes

nUSDC backs every market, so the Collateral Vault is curated for safety first and interest second. It lends USDC only to established, low-risk places where loans are overcollateralised and you can get money back quickly. It does not chase high rates.

| Kind of allocation | Example | Why it's considered safe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue-chip crypto loans | USDC lent on Morpho against ETH, wstETH or wrapped bitcoin | Borrowers post more than they borrow, and liquid collateral can be sold quickly if prices fall. |
| Established lending pools | USDC supplied to long-running money markets such as Aave | Deep liquidity and a long track record. |
| Tokenised Treasuries | USDC lent against tokenised short-term US government debt | Collateral backed by short-dated government bonds, with steady prices. |

These are examples of the kind of place the vault lends to, not a fixed list. The curator sets the actual allocations and caps, and they change over time. You can see them onchain at any moment.

Safe is not the same as risk-free. Each allocation has its own smart contract, collateral and liquidity risks, and the vault inherits all of them.

## Who gets the interest

It depends on what your nUSDC is doing.

| Your nUSDC is | The interest goes to |
| --- | --- |
| In a Receiver pool | Payers. That is what following a price costs. See [Payers earn interest and fees](/how-null-works/payers-earn-interest-and-fees). |
| In a Payer pool | You, plus the Receivers' interest. |
| In the Savings Vault | You, plus what borrowers on the long markets pay. |
| Posted as Margin collateral | You. It keeps earning while it sits in Morpho. |

## What to watch

If the Collateral Vault loses value, everything built on it loses with it: every pool, the Savings Vault and every Morpho market. The vault's curator and allocations deserve the same scrutiny as Null's own contracts.
