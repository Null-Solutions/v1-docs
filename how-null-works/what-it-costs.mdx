Three costs can show up around a position. A market may charge a fee when you enter or leave. Your position earns or gives up interest while you hold it. And Ethereum charges gas whenever you transact. Only the first is a Null fee.

## Entry and exit fees

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Who sets it | Each market has its own **Entry fee** and **Exit fee**, shown in its market information. One may charge nothing. |
| The cap | Neither fee can go above 2.5%. The factory owner can adjust fees within that cap, and a change never rewrites trades already made. |
| How it's taken | The fee comes out of the amount you trade. The review screen says **Protocol fee included in this purchase** or **in this sale**. |
| What it's charged on | Notional: the size of the position you open or close. |
| Where it goes | It depends on your side. A Receiver's opening and closing fees are paid to the Payers. A Payer's fees go to the protocol. |

## Why Payer fees look bigger

For a Receiver, notional is roughly what you put in. A Payer backs the whole Receiver pool, so its notional is scaled by `R / P`. The same fee rate is charged on a bigger position.

| You post | Side | `R / P` | Notional | Fee at 0.25% |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1,000 | Receiver | any | about 1,000 | 2.50 |
| 1,000 | Payer | 1 | about 1,000 | 2.50 |
| 1,000 | Payer | 5 | about 5,000 | 12.50 |
| 1,000 | Payer | 10 | about 10,000 | 25.00 |

This is not a penalty for choosing Payer. It is the same rate on a bigger position.

## A round trip

Buy 1,000 of Receiver exposure in a market with a 0.25% entry fee and a 0.25% exit fee. About 2.49 comes out on the way in, leaving 997.51 in the pool. If nothing else changes, about 2.49 comes out on the way back. You finish with about 995.02 before gas, a round trip of about half a percent.

## Holding is free of Null fees

Null charges nothing while you hold a pool position. That doesn't make holding free in every sense:

| You hold | What it costs or pays while you hold |
| --- | --- |
| Receiver | You give up the interest on your deposit. It goes to Payers. |
| Payer | You earn the interest on both pools' deposits. |
| Savings | No extra app fee. You earn the **AER**. |
| Lending | No Null cut of the **Lend Rate**. |
| Margin | Entry and exit fees when the desk buys and sells, plus the borrow interest on your loan. |

## At the review screen

Check the **Entry fee** or **Exit fee**, then the amount after the fee. That is the position you are really opening or closing. Then set the minimum you will accept. Settlement runs inside your transaction, so pools may have moved since the preview. If the result would be worse than your minimum, the transaction fails and the gas is still spent.

Contract level detail is in [Fees](/protocol/fees).
