Null uses Morpho Vault V2 shares in two product roles. Understanding both makes Savings, Spot, Lending, and Margin easier to read in the app.

## Two roles

| Role | What it is | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Collateral vault** | The ERC-4626 Morpho vault share used as a Null market's `collateral` (for example `nmUSDC`) | Backs Receiver and Payer pools. Earns Morpho Blue lending yield ("safe yield" relative to holding idle cash). That yield is the source of [funding](/learn/funding-and-carry). |
| **Null Savings Vault** | The same Morpho Vault V2, shown in the app as **Null Savings Vault** | Lets you deposit USDC from Cash. The vault allocates that USDC across Morpho Blue **long** markets that lend against Null receiver-pool shares. |

On the current Morpho loop stack, those roles share one vault address. Spot markets still hold vault shares in the pools. Savings is how you deposit the underlying (USDC) into that vault without opening a Receiver or Payer position.

## Collateral vault (market collateral)

Every Null market is created with one ERC-20 collateral token. On the Morpho-integrated deployment that token is a Morpho Vault V2 share (`nmUSDC`) whose underlying is USDC.

- Both pools account in vault-share units.
- When Morpho Blue borrowers pay interest, vault share value rises.
- Settlement reconciles that growth across Receiver and Payer.
- Receivers pay a share of that yield to Payers as funding.

Older mainnet markets may still use another yield-bearing vault (for example Steakhouse Prime USDC / `steakUSDC`). Always verify the market's collateral on the [addresses page](/resources/addresses) and in the app.

## Null Savings Vault

In the app, **Savings** is USDC you deposit into the Morpho vault. You receive vault shares. Interest accrues into share value; it is not paid as a separate transfer.

The vault does **not** deposit straight into Null receiver pools. It allocates USDC through its Morpho Market V1 adapter into Morpho Blue **long** markets:

- **Loan token:** USDC
- **Collateral token:** that Null market's **receiver** pool share

Those Blue markets are the same long markets leveraged traders borrow from when they open a Morpho long on Null. Savings lenders earn the Morpho supply rate on those books. The Savings card shows that rate as an annual equivalent rate (AER).

Most of the vault is lent out, so a large withdrawal needs free USDC (idle liquidity). If free liquidity is short, withdraw a smaller amount or wait for more capacity.

## How the two roles meet

```text
USDC (Cash)
   │
   ├─ Savings deposit ──► Morpho Vault V2 (nmUSDC)
   │                         │
   │                         └─ allocates to Morpho Blue long markets
   │                              (USDC lent, receiver shares as borrower collateral)
   │
   └─ Spot / Payer mint ──► wrap to nmUSDC ──► Null Receiver or Payer pool
```

- **Savings** earns Morpho lend APY from leveraged longs and other borrowers on those Blue markets.
- **Spot** holds nmUSDC in Null pools and tracks the oracle asset; funding moves vault yield from Receivers to Payers.
- **Margin longs** borrow the USDC that Savings (and other lenders) supply.

See [Morpho](/learn/morpho) for long and short market topology, and [Collateral](/markets/collateral) for deposit unit rules.
