Funding on Null is not a separate rate that floats against an external perpetual index. **Collateral APR is the funding source.** Receivers earn the vault yield on their pool collateral, then pay a fixed share of that yield to Payers as funding. Payers do not pay funding back the other way.

In the app, that share is treated as the full collateral APR on the Receiver pool (`s = 1`): Receivers keep price exposure and Payers receive the Receiver pool's collateral yield as funding, scaled by how large Receiver is relative to Payer.

## What you see in the app

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Collateral APR** | Estimated annualized yield of the Morpho vault share used as market collateral. Both pools accrue it through reconciliation. |
| **Funding APR** | The share of Receiver-side collateral yield paid to Payers. In current product copy it equals Collateral APR when the vault yield is positive, and is never negative. |
| **Net APR** | Side-adjusted carry: collateral yield plus funding received, or minus funding paid. On Payer, funding is scaled by `R / P`. |

Hover copy in the markets UI matches this model: both sides accrue collateral yield; Receivers pay part of it to Payers as funding.

## How the transfer works

Let:

- `y` be the collateral (vault) APR;
- `s` be the yield share paid as funding (`0 ≤ s ≤ 1`; the app uses `s = 1`);
- `R` be Receiver pool assets;
- `P` be Payer pool assets.

Then:

```text
funding APR f = s * y     (never negative in the product model)
annual funding transfer = f * R   (Receiver → Payer)
```

Ignoring price PnL and fees:

```text
Receiver net carry ≈ y - f = (1 - s) * y
Payer net carry    ≈ y + f * (R / P)
```

With `s = 1`, Receivers keep price exposure but do not keep the vault yield on their notional; that yield is the funding Payers earn. Payers still also earn collateral yield on their own pool shares.

<Note>
  Funding is variable because vault yield is variable. It is not a fixed coupon
  and it is not a promise of profit after hedge costs.
</Note>

## Worked example

Suppose collateral APR `y = 8%`, `s = 1`, `R = 1,000`, and `P = 200`:

```text
f = 8%
annual funding transfer = 8% × 1,000 = 80   (Receiver → Payer)

Receiver net carry ≈ 8% - 8% = 0%   (before price PnL)
Payer net carry    ≈ 8% + 8% × (1,000 / 200) = 8% + 40% = 48%
```

A Payer holder with position value `p = 50` (25% of the Payer pool) receives 25% of the Payer-side funding transfer, so about `20` annualized before elapsed-time prorating and settlement caps.

## Settlement still uses an FRM address

Each market identity still includes an `frm` address: `(collateral, oracle, frm)`. Settlement asks that contract for a rate, then applies funding on settleable Receiver notional together with price PnL.

For product and UI purposes, treat funding as **yield share of collateral APR**, not as a separate signed imbalance curve that can flip so Payers pay Receivers. Lens still returns both `fundingRate` and `collateralRate` so integrators can display them separately.

See [Settlement](/developers/settlement) for order of operations, and [Frontend integration](/contracts/integration/ui) for display formulas.

## Collateral yield vs funding

Yield-bearing collateral (typically a Morpho Vault V2 share such as `nmUSDC`) appreciates as Morpho Blue borrowers pay interest. Reconciliation lifts both pools when the market's external collateral balance grows.

Funding is how part of the **Receiver** pool's claim on that yield is transferred to the **Payer** pool. It is not a second external yield source.

For how Savings and market collateral relate, see [Vaults](/learn/vaults).

## Payer hedging

Market makers who hold Payer and hedge the tracked asset elsewhere care about net carry after hedge costs. With yield-share funding, the funding leg they earn is driven by vault APR and `R / P`, not by an imbalance target.

Let a market maker own `p` collateral units of a Payer pool whose total assets are `P`. With instantaneous leverage `L = R / P` and oracle price `S`:

```text
h = pL / S = (p / P) * (R / S) base units
H = hS = pL = pR / P quote notional
```

The hedge is optional and external. See [Market making](/private-beta/market-make/overview).
