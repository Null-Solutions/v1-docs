Null's Margin product opens leveraged exposure through **Morpho Blue**, not through a Null-native margin account. The periphery contract is `NullV1MorphoRouter`. App config pins which Null markets have a long loop, a short market, or both (`loopMarkets` / `SHORT_MARKETS` in the UI).

Spot Receiver and Payer positions stay fully collateralized inside Null pools. Morpho liquidations apply only to **leveraged Morpho positions**, when loan-to-value reaches the market's LLTV.

## Two Morpho markets per Null market

For a Null market that is fully wired:

| Morpho market | Loan token | Collateral token | Who uses it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Long (loop)** | USDC (vault underlying) | Receiver pool share (`rTOKEN`) | Leveraged longs; Savings vault allocates USDC here |
| **Short** | Receiver pool share | Null collateral vault share (`nmUSDC`) | Leveraged shorts; Lending can supply receiver shares here |

Each Blue market has its own oracle, IRM, and **LLTV** (liquidation loan-to-value).

Typical configured LLTVs on the current loop stack:

| Market | Long LLTV | Short LLTV | App leverage slider (under LLTV) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ETH | 62.5% | 91.5% | Long ~2.4×; Short up to ~10× |
| AAPL / DEMO | 91.5% | 91.5% | Up to ~10× |

Max theoretical leverage at full open LTV is `1 / (1 − LLTV)`. The app slider stays below that. For a comparison of LLTV drop-to-liquidation versus perpetuals-style maintenance margin, see the internal note under `liquidation-compare/` in this docs repo.

## Long market (loop)

A long opens leveraged **Receiver** exposure in one transaction:

1. You post USDC equity.
2. `NullV1MorphoRouter.open` flash-borrows the rest of the USDC notional from Morpho.
3. The router wraps to `nmUSDC`, deposits into the Null **receiver** pool, and posts the receiver shares as Morpho collateral.
4. It borrows USDC on Morpho to repay the flash loan.

You end long receiver exposure with Morpho debt in USDC. Closing reverses the path (`close`): repay debt, withdraw collateral, redeem receiver shares, unwrap to USDC.

**Risk:** Morpho can liquidate if LTV reaches LLTV (oracle moves, interest accrual, or collateral value changes). Spot Receiver holders who never opened a Morpho loop are not liquidated by Morpho.

## Short market

A short opens negative receiver exposure:

1. You post USDC equity; the router wraps it to `nmUSDC`.
2. `openShort` supplies `nmUSDC` as Morpho collateral and borrows receiver shares.
3. The router redeems those shares in the Null receiver pool (selling the synthetic exposure).

Closing (`closeShort`) repays the share debt, withdraws collateral, and returns leftover USDC. Short closes mint just enough receiver shares to repay debt and do not depend on spare lender liquidity on the short book.

**Risk:** same Morpho LLTV liquidation model on the short Blue market.

## NullV1MorphoRouter

`NullV1MorphoRouter` is a stateless periphery helper around Morpho Blue and Null pools:

- Long: `open` / `close` (and Permit2 variants where deployed)
- Short: `openShort` / `closeShort`
- Caller must authorize the router on Morpho (`setAuthorization`) before borrow / withdraw-collateral flows
- Factory whitelist and deposit-pause rules still apply when the router mints or redeems pool shares

Oracles:

- **Long:** `NullV1MorphoOracle` prices receiver shares for Morpho
- **Short:** `NullV1MorphoInverseOracle` prices the inverse relationship for share loans against `nmUSDC`

## Lending and Savings touch the same books

- **Lending** in the app can supply USDC into long Morpho markets or supply receiver shares into short Morpho markets.
- **Null Savings Vault** allocates primarily into the long markets' USDC lending side (see [Vaults](/learn/vaults)).

Idle USDC on a long market is what new longs borrow. The Savings vault supplies that liquidity; it does not replace Morpho lenders entirely, and thin free USDC can block large long opens or Savings withdrawals.
