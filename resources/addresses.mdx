## Indexer Source

The public indexer tracks the Sepolia `NullV1Factory` from block `10976251`.

Dynamic markets are discovered from the factory's `MarketCreated` event. Each market deploys its own Receiver pool and Payer pool, so pool addresses should be read from the market, factory events, Lens responses, or the indexer rather than hardcoded.

## What To Hardcode

Hardcode shared infrastructure addresses like `NullV1Factory`, `NullV1Router`, `NullV1Lens`, and `NullV1Multicall` per chain.

Do not hardcode market pool addresses unless you intentionally pin to one specific market. Receiver and Payer pools are deployed per market and should normally be discovered from `NullV1Factory`, `NullV1Market`, `NullV1Lens`, or indexer data.

See [Integrator Quickstart](/developers-tab/integrator-quickstart), [Router](/developers-tab/router), and [Indexer Integration](/contracts/integration/indexer) for how these addresses fit into a full integration.
