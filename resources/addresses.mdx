Null V1 targets **Ethereum mainnet**. Canonical contract addresses and the factory deployment block will be published here after deployment verification.

## Status

| Network | Chain ID | Status | Explorer |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Ethereum mainnet | `1` | Deployment addresses pending | [Etherscan](https://etherscan.io) |

## Canonical contracts

This page will publish verified values for:

- `NullV1Factory`;
- `NullV1Router`;
- `NullV1PayableRouter`;
- enabled funding-rate models;
- `NullV1Lens`;
- `NullV1Multicall`;
- supported collateral and oracle contracts; and
- the factory deployment block for indexer backfills.

<Warning>
Do not deploy an integration with guessed, copied, or unverified addresses. Wait for the Ethereum mainnet table on this page and verify every address on Etherscan.
</Warning>

## Integration rules

Hardcode verified shared infrastructure for Ethereum mainnet only after it is published here. Discover markets from `CreateMarket` or `factory.getMarket(collateral, oracle, frm)`.

Do not derive or preselect Receiver and Payer pool addresses. Read them from the market, Factory event, Lens, or your own indexer.
