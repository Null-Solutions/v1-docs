## Indexer source

Index the Sepolia factory from [block `11321037`](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/block/11321037). Markets come from `CreateMarket`; each market deploys its own Receiver and Payer pools—read those from events, the market, Lens, or your indexer. Do not hardcode pool addresses unless you intentionally pin one market.

<Note>
Deployed Sepolia Lens exposes core `previewState` / `simulateState` / `previewMarket` / `simulateMarket`. Confirm the deployed ABI before using newer collateral-metric helpers from latest source.
</Note>

## What to hardcode

Hardcode factory, both routers, enabled FRM, Lens, and Multicall per chain.

Do not hardcode market pool addresses unless pinning a specific market. Discover pools from Factory, Market, Lens, or indexer data.
