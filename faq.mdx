Most questions about Null come down to a few distinctions: pool shares versus the assets they
track, funded products versus leveraged ones, and rates that describe the present versus
returns nobody has promised. The answers below take those distinctions one at a time.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Null?">
    Null is a market built from two pools that take opposite sides of an oracle tracked price,
    with Savings, Lending, and Margin arranged around that core. If you are new to the design,
    [the primitive](/concepts/the-primitive) explains how the two pools work together.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I own the stock or token a market tracks?">
    No. You own a share of a collateral pool, a share of a vault, or a supply position on Morpho,
    depending on the product you used. No voting rights and no dividends reach you directly,
    although an oracle whose return includes distributions reflects them in its price.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Savings and Lending?">
    Savings is the managed route, where **Add money** deposits USDC into the Null Savings Vault
    and the vault lends across long books for you. Lending is the direct route, where you pick
    a book yourself and use **Lend** and **Recall**, including Receiver share books.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Spot and Margin?">
    Spot buys you an unlevered share of a pool, so your position can lose value but can never be
    liquidated. Margin borrows against collateral on Morpho to enlarge the position, and that
    borrowing introduces a liquidation threshold at the book's LLTV.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is Payer, and why can I not see it?">
    Payer is hidden until you turn on **Pro Mode** in the account menu. Once it is on, **Payer
    buy** and **Payer sell** appear on the Spot ticket. Payer backs Receiver and moves against
    the oracle price, so the app keeps it out of sight until you ask for it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is funding always paid to Payers?">
    Funding is the yield earned on collateral inside the Receiver pool, routed to Payers as
    payment for backing Receiver exposure. The direction never flips, even when Payer collateral
    is larger than Receiver collateral, though coverage changes how much each Payer earns.
    [Funding and yield](/concepts/funding-and-yield) works through the numbers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Net APR actually mean?">
    Net APR combines collateral yield with funding, signed for the side you hold, and on a Payer
    position it is scaled by `R / P`. It describes what current rates imply right now. It is not
    a quote anyone has made you, and it does not predict your profit at exit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not withdraw all of my Savings?">
    Most of the vault's USDC is out on loan to Margin longs at any given moment, so only the
    unlent remainder is free to leave immediately. The panel shows how much that is. As loans are
    repaid or positions close, more of the balance becomes withdrawable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Receive do?">
    **Receive** displays your connected wallet's address and a QR code so you, an exchange, or
    another wallet can send assets to it. That is all it does. It opens no position and commits
    no funds, and assets become Cash only once they arrive in the wallet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are points a form of yield?">
    No. Season 0 monitors ordinary trading activity, and points record that activity without
    changing the value of any pool share, position, or settlement. They carry no stated value
    and promise no token or payout. See [Points](/points) and the [Legal notices](/legal).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which markets are live right now?">
    The list on `/trade` is the live set, and it is the only list worth trusting. These docs
    deliberately avoid naming symbols, because the indexer decides what is listed and that set
    can change without the documentation changing with it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Has Null been audited?">
    Nethermind reviewed an earlier, defined scope of the core contracts, and that review has
    real limits. Later contracts, including Margin routing and the vault, can sit outside it.
    Read [Audits](/protocol/audits) for what was covered before relying on it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
