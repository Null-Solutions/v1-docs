## Material risks

Using Null involves significant risk, including:

- **position risk:** Receiver and Payer values can fall substantially;
- **Payer short scale:** a Payer is a negative-size position in the Receiver pool; lower coverage increases Payer's percentage exposure;
- **funding risk:** funding is paid from variable collateral yield;
- **Morpho and Margin risk:** leveraged positions can be liquidated at LLTV;
- **smart-contract risk:** bugs or unintended behavior can cause loss;
- **oracle risk:** incorrect, stale, unavailable, or divergent prices can affect or block settlement;
- **collateral and vault risk:** collateral or the Morpho vault can lose value or become difficult to redeem;
- **liquidity risk:** entry, exit, transfer, Savings withdrawal, lending recall, or hedging liquidity may be unavailable;
- **execution risk:** settlement, slippage, deadlines, gas, failed transactions, and other flows can change outcomes;
- **external-venue risk:** a hedge can incur funding, basis, margin, liquidation, custody, or operational losses; and
- **regulatory and tax risk:** treatment can vary by participant and jurisdiction and may change.

Nethermind Security reviewed a defined scope of Null V1 contracts. A completed audit, immutability, and onchain visibility do not guarantee correct code or safe outcomes. Later contracts, including Margin routing, can sit outside that report.

Read [Risks](/concepts/risks) and [Audits](/protocol/audits).
