Welcome to Null 101 — your starting point for understanding the Null V1 protocol.

## What is Null?

Null is a perpetual return swaps protocol that eliminates liquidations through bounded pool settlement.

## Core Concepts

- **Receiver and Payer roles** — the two sides of every market
- **ERC-4626 share tokens** — how positions are represented
- **Bounded settlement** — how the protocol stays solvent without liquidations
