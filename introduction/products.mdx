# Ways to use Null

Each Null V1 market has two sides. Choose the side that matches the exposure and funding profile you want, then use the pool or router flow to enter.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Receive the reference return" icon="arrow-trend-up" href="/learn/receiver-and-payer">
    Receiver shares are synthetic long exposure to the reference price. Receivers pay funding to Payers for the backing capital they use.

    Best for users who want oracle-referenced upside exposure without holding the reference asset.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Pay and earn funding" icon="vault" href="/learn/funding-and-carry">
    Payer shares back Receiver exposure, receive funding, and take the opposite price risk.

    Best for market makers or users who want the Payer funding stream and understand the price risk.
  </Card>
</Columns>
