# Null V1

Null V1 is a non-custodial protocol for creating oracle-referenced synthetic exposure with pooled collateral. Each market has a Receiver pool and a Payer pool. Receivers get the reference asset return and pay funding. Payers back that exposure, receive funding, and take the opposite price risk.

The core invariant is full collateralization. The protocol can redistribute collateral between pools, but it cannot create debt or an undercollateralized receivable.

## How Null fits together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Markets are pooled">
    A market aggregates all Receiver capital into one pool and all Payer capital into another. Users hold ERC-4626 compatible shares in the side they choose.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Updates happen before actions">
    Before deposits, mints, withdrawals, or redemptions, the market validates the oracle price, accounts for yield-fee/share-price changes when enabled, reconciles collateral, and settles funding plus price movement.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Settlement is bounded">
    Price settlement and funding are netted into one transfer. The paying pool can never pay more than it has.
  </Step>
</Steps>
