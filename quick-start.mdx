Null V1 markets have two sides. Receiver shares get synthetic long exposure to the reference price. Payer shares back that exposure and take the opposite price risk. Funding is signed: Receiver pays Payer below the FRM's target Payer coverage, and Payer pays Receiver above it.

Your first position is four steps away.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Choose a side" icon="split">
    Pick Receiver for synthetic exposure, or Payer to back the market. Which side earns funding depends on Payer coverage relative to the FRM target.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Bring collateral" icon="wallet">
    Use the supported collateral for the market. On Sepolia, see the live deployment page for current test collateral.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Mint shares" icon="sparkles">
    Deposit through the router and receive ERC-4626 pool shares in your wallet.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Receiver or Payer">
    Read the [protocol overview](/learn/overview) if you are unsure which side fits your goal.

    - Choose **Receiver** if you want reference-asset upside and accept downside risk plus signed funding.
    - Choose **Payer** if you want to back Receiver exposure and accept the opposite price risk plus signed funding.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the market">
    A market is identified by `(collateral, oracle, frm)`. Use the app, an indexer, or `NullV1Factory.getMarket(collateral, oracle, frm)` to find the market address. The funding-rate model (FRM) contains the market's funding parameters.

    Use [Live Deployments](/resources/addresses) for the current factory, router, lens, and supported network details.

    Once you have the market, read `receiverPool()` and `payerPool()` to get the pool addresses.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview the action">
    Use the target pool's ERC-4626 preview function before sending a transaction. Pool previews take market-collateral units. If the market collateral is itself an ERC-4626 vault, router amounts are instead denominated in that vault's underlying, so quote the vault conversion before the pool preview.

    ```ts
    const shares = await receiverPool.previewDeposit(receiverAssets);
    ```

    Previews are estimates. Execution updates the market first, so the final amount can change if price, funding, collateral balance, or fees change before your transaction lands.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deposit with protection">
    For most users and integrations, call the router selected for the market's oracle. The routers support Receiver and Payer deposits in one transaction, ERC-4626 collateral wrapping, slippage minimums, deadlines, and optional oracle `updateData`.

    ```ts
    await router.depositMarket(
      market,
      receiverAssets,
      payerAssets,
      minReceiverShares,
      minPayerShares,
      deadline,
      updateData
    );
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you deposit

Your position is your ERC-20 share balance in the Receiver pool, the Payer pool, or both. The value of those shares changes when the market updates.
