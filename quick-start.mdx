# Quick start

Null V1 markets have two sides. Receiver shares get synthetic long exposure to the reference price and pay funding. Payer shares back that exposure, receive funding, and take the opposite price risk.

Use this page as the shortest path from zero to a position.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Receiver or Payer">
    Read [Receiver and Payer](/learn/receiver-and-payer) if you are unsure which side fits your goal.

    - Choose **Receiver** if you want reference-asset upside and accept funding cost plus downside risk.
    - Choose **Payer** if you want the funding stream and accept the opposite price risk.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the market">
    A market is identified by `(asset, oracle, mu)`. Use the app, an indexer, or `NullV1Factory.getMarket(asset, oracle, mu)` to find the market address.

    Once you have the market, read `receiverPool()` and `payerPool()` to get the pool addresses.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview the action">
    Use the target pool's ERC-4626 preview function before sending a transaction.

    ```ts
    const shares = await receiverPool.previewDeposit(receiverAssets);
    ```

    Previews are estimates. Execution updates the market first, so the final amount can change if price, funding, collateral balance, or fees change before your transaction lands.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deposit with protection">
    For most users and integrations, call `NullV1Router.depositMarket`. The router supports Receiver and Payer deposits in one transaction, slippage minimums, deadlines, optional oracle `updateData`, and ERC-4626 collateral wrapping.

    ```ts
    await router.depositMarket(
      market,
      receiverAssets,
      payerAssets,
      minReceiverShares,
      minPayerShares,
      deadline,
      updateData
    );
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you deposit

Your position is your ERC-20 share balance in the Receiver pool, the Payer pool, or both. The value of those shares changes when the market updates.
