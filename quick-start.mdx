Null V1 is an on-chain perpetuals protocol built around two complementary liquidity pools per market. Before you send your first transaction, it helps to understand how the two sides interact, where to find a market address, and how the router protects you from slippage and stale prices. This guide walks you through every step from a standing start to a successful deposit.

## Next steps

Now that you have shares in a pool, read the full [Depositing guide](/depositing) to understand every parameter in depth — including how to deposit into a single pool, how to handle push-oracle `updateData`, and how slippage errors surface when the on-chain price moves against you.
