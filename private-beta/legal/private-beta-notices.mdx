Entry and exit fees, when set, are charged on notional and usually credit the other pool. They are not paid to Null unless the factory was deployed with protocol-fee routing. The annualized collateral fee is disabled.

You remain responsible for Ethereum gas and any wallet, transfer, onramp, off-ramp, collateral, oracle, venue, hedging, tax, or third-party costs. Funding transfers between Receiver and Payer are part of protocol settlement; they are not fees paid to Null.

## Material risks

Using Null involves significant risk, including:

- **position risk:** Receiver and Payer values can fall substantially;
- **Payer leverage:** lower coverage increases Payer's percentage exposure;
- **funding risk:** funding is paid from variable collateral APR;
- **Morpho / Margin risk:** leveraged loops can be liquidated at LLTV;
- **smart-contract risk:** bugs or unintended behavior can cause loss;
- **oracle risk:** incorrect, stale, unavailable, or divergent prices can affect or block settlement;
- **collateral and vault risk:** collateral or an underlying Morpho vault can lose value or become difficult to redeem;
- **liquidity risk:** entry, exit, transfer, Savings withdrawal, or hedging liquidity may be unavailable;
- **execution risk:** settlement, slippage, deadlines, gas, failed transactions, and other flows can change outcomes;
- **external-venue risk:** a hedge can incur funding, basis, margin, liquidation, custody, or operational losses; and
- **regulatory and tax risk:** treatment can vary by participant and jurisdiction and may change.

Nethermind Security reviewed the Null V1 contracts. A completed audit, immutability, and onchain visibility do not guarantee correct code or safe outcomes.

Read [Risks](/private-beta/getting-started/before-you-trade), [Risks and parameters](/learn/risks-and-parameters), and [Audit status](/private-beta/support/audits).
