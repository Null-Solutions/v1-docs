## Technical test only

The private beta is a limited, invitation-only software test. Its purpose is to evaluate the UI and protocol through practical use, including whether Receiver and Payer pools can attract enough test capital and activity to validate accounting, settlement, liquidity, oracle, and user-experience behavior under realistic conditions.

The private beta is not a commercial service or a public launch. Null may change, restrict, suspend, or end the UI, protocol access, markets, features, eligibility criteria, or test at any time. There is no expectation that the private beta or any feature will continue.

Participants are expected to evaluate the software and provide technical and operational feedback. Participation does not create a right to future access, products, services, tokens, allocations, rewards, or compensation.

## Restricted access

Access is limited to participants approved by Null who have completed the Private Beta Agreement. Approval applies to the accepted participant, sign-in account, and wallet. It does not transfer automatically to another person, account, or wallet.

The private beta is not offered to persons in the United Kingdom. It is also unavailable wherever access or participation would breach applicable law, sanctions, or the Private Beta Agreement. Do not apply, access the UI, or participate if you are ineligible.

An invitation, referral link, signed application, or displayed approval status is not a public offer and does not guarantee access. Null may reject or revoke access in accordance with the Private Beta Agreement.

## Self-directed and non-custodial

You connect and control your own wallet. You decide whether to submit each transaction and sign it yourself. Null does not:

- hold your private keys or recovery credentials;
- custody or control assets in your wallet;
- exercise discretion over your orders or positions;
- guarantee that a transaction will execute or settle as previewed; or
- recover assets, reverse transactions, or compensate losses.

Transactions interact with smart contracts. Once confirmed onchain, they may be irreversible.

## No protocol fees to Null

The private-beta entry fee, exit fee, and annualized collateral fee are set to **0%**. Null does not receive transaction compensation from private-beta protocol activity.

You remain responsible for Ethereum gas and any wallet, transfer, onramp, off-ramp, collateral, oracle, venue, hedging, tax, or third-party costs. Funding transfers between Receiver and Payer are part of protocol settlement; they are not fees paid to Null.

## NullPoints are not financial returns

NullPoints are non-transferable accounting entries used to measure private-beta participation and help assemble the capital and usage needed for technical testing. They do not increase pool-share value or change settlement.

NullPoints are not money, a cryptoasset, interest, yield, a fee rebate, a claim against Null, or a promise of any token, allocation, conversion, cash value, or payout. Null may change or discontinue points, invitation thresholds, referral attribution, or eligibility at any time.

See [Points and referrals](/private-beta/status-and-points/points-and-referrals) for the current preview mechanics.

## Material risks

Private-beta participation involves significant risk, including:

- **position risk** — Receiver and Payer values can fall substantially;
- **Payer leverage** — lower coverage increases Payer's percentage exposure;
- **funding risk** — funding is variable and can reverse;
- **smart-contract risk** — bugs or unintended behavior can cause loss;
- **oracle risk** — incorrect, stale, unavailable, or divergent prices can affect or block settlement;
- **collateral risk** — collateral or an underlying vault can lose value or become difficult to redeem;
- **liquidity risk** — entry, exit, transfer, or hedging liquidity may be unavailable;
- **execution risk** — settlement, slippage, deadlines, gas, failed transactions, and other flows can change outcomes;
- **external-venue risk** — a hedge can incur funding, basis, margin, liquidation, custody, or operational losses; and
- **regulatory and tax risk** — treatment can vary by participant and jurisdiction and may change.

Null V1 has not completed a published third-party audit. Immutability and onchain visibility do not guarantee correct code or safe outcomes.

Read [Before you trade](/private-beta/getting-started/before-you-trade), [Risks and parameters](/learn/risks-and-parameters), and [Audit status](/private-beta/support/audits).
