Approved, signed-in participants can open `/referrals` to view their referral code, copy an invite link, and see their current successful-referral total.

## Share an invite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Referrals">
    Sign in and go to `/referrals`. Your wallet has its own referral code.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy the invite link">
    Use the copy control next to your code. The link opens the beta application with your code filled in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send it to an invitee">
    The invitee can keep the prefilled code while completing their details and Beta Participation Agreement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What counts as successful

A referral counts when a distinct invited wallet:

- applies with your referral code;
- completes the Beta Participation Agreement; and
- receives private-beta wallet access.

Your own wallet cannot count as its own referral. The displayed total can update after the invitee completes the required steps and their access is processed.

<Note>
The app currently displays successful referrals as a count. It does not document or promise a separate points value, payout, or conversion for each referral.
</Note>

See [Status & Points](/private-beta/status-and-points) for the current display behavior.
