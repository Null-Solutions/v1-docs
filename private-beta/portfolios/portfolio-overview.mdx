After signing in, use your portfolio to review the activity associated with your connected wallet.

## What you can view

- **Balances** — wallet assets available to fund positions.
- **Positions or Minted** — Receiver and Payer pool shares held by your wallet.
- **History** — recorded position activity.
- **Watchlist** — markets you saved. This tab appears when the watchlist is not empty.
- **Portfolio summary** — the displayed portfolio value and performance information.

Select a position or market to open its trading page. Use **Mint** to add to a position and **Burn** to redeem shares.

Deposit and withdrawal controls manage wallet funds. They do not mint or burn a market position.

<Note>
Displayed portfolio values are estimates, not guaranteed redemption amounts.
</Note>
