## Cannot enter the beta

Confirm that you signed in with the account and wallet used for your application. A signed agreement means the application was received. An administrator must still approve that wallet onchain before protected features become available.

If you changed accounts or wallets, return to the approved combination and sign in again.

## Market is unavailable

A market can be visible while a transaction is unavailable. Check the trading panel for:

- an **online**, **closed**, or **missing Payer funds** state;
- missing or unavailable price data; and
- the selected market, side, and collateral.

Ordinary stale oracle periods block settlement-dependent actions. Wait for valid price data instead of repeatedly submitting the same transaction. A circuit-breaker event is different: it skips price PnL for that update but does not, by itself, revert settlement.

## Cannot mint

Check that:

1. the market is available and has current price data;
2. your connected wallet is the approved wallet;
3. the wallet holds enough of the market's collateral, not only another token;
4. you selected Receiver or Payer and entered an amount; and
5. you confirmed each required approval and transaction request.

Funding your wallet does not mint a position. After funds arrive, return to the market and submit **Mint** separately.

## Cannot burn

Confirm that the connected wallet holds shares in the selected Receiver or Payer pool. The amount cannot exceed that pool-share balance. A wallet balance, portfolio estimate, or position on the other side is not a burnable balance for the selected pool.

Burn output reflects the pool's value after settlement. It can differ from an earlier estimate because funding, price PnL, fees, and reconciliation run before redemption.

## Transaction expired or reverted

Null's router protects private-beta transactions with deadlines and minimum outputs. Documented contract errors include:

- `TransactionTooOld` — the transaction executed after its deadline. Refresh the quote and submit a new transaction.
- `SlippageReceiverShares` or `SlippagePayerShares` — a mint would return fewer shares than the submitted minimum.
- `SlippageReceiverAssets` or `SlippagePayerAssets` — a burn would return fewer assets than the submitted minimum.
- `StalePrice` — the oracle price is outside its ordinary freshness window.

Refresh the market state before retrying. Do not keep resubmitting a slippage or stale-price failure without obtaining a current preview.

`InsufficientUpdateFeeBalance` refers to the payable router's native-token balance for an oracle update. It does not mean your collateral balance is too low. Report it with the market and transaction details.

## Transaction is pending or missing

After confirmation in your wallet, wait for the transaction to confirm onchain. If the app has not updated:

1. copy the transaction hash from your wallet;
2. check whether it is pending, successful, or reverted;
3. confirm that the connected wallet still matches the transaction sender; and
4. refresh the portfolio after confirmation.

Avoid submitting the same action again until you know the first transaction's status.

## Funding has not arrived

Confirm that the faucet transaction succeeded on Sepolia and that `MockUSDC` arrived in the approved wallet. `MockUSDC`, `nUSDC`, and Sepolia ETH are separate assets. The faucet does not provide Sepolia ETH for gas.

Do not send mainnet USDC or other production assets to a Sepolia token contract. Testnet assets have no cash value.

## Get help

Use the Telegram link shown after your beta application. Include:

- the page route and market address;
- the connected wallet address;
- the transaction hash, if one exists;
- whether the transaction is pending, successful, or reverted; and
- the exact error text or a screenshot with sensitive information removed.

Never share a seed phrase, private key, password, one-time code, or wallet recovery secret. Support does not need them.
