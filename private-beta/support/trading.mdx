## Cannot enter the beta

Confirm that you signed in with the account and wallet used for your application. Agreement completion and on-chain wallet approval are separate.

## Cannot mint

Check that:

1. the market is available;
2. current price data is available;
3. your wallet holds enough of the market's collateral;
4. you selected Receiver or Payer; and
5. any approval and transaction requests were confirmed.

## Cannot burn

Confirm that the connected wallet holds shares in the selected pool and that the entered amount does not exceed the available balance.

## Funding has not arrived

Crypto bridging and cash onramp transfers can take time. Check the provider status and destination wallet before retrying.

For community help, use the Telegram link shown after your beta application. Include the route, market address, transaction hash when available, and the exact error. Never share private credentials.
