---

A self-service market-making program is not currently available in the private-beta app.

The app does not provide market-maker onboarding, quoted-order controls, API credentials, or an order-book workflow.

If the Null team has invited you to discuss liquidity support, use the contact channel provided with that invitation. Do not send funds or sign transactions based on an unsolicited message.

<Warning>
Null's Receiver and Payer pools are not a conventional order book. General order-book market-making instructions do not apply.
</Warning>
