## Current status

Treat Null V1 as unaudited.

No completed security audit or formal-verification report is published in these docs. This page will link reports if they become available. Until then, do not assume that a contract, router, oracle integration, or deployed market has received an independent review.

<Warning>
Private-beta access, testing, a small codebase, and protective contract controls are not substitutes for an audit. An unaudited protocol carries additional risk.
</Warning>

## What an audit would not guarantee

Even a completed audit would not prove that the protocol has no bugs or that a position is safe. Audit scope and deployment details matter. Oracle behavior, collateral risk, market parameters, integrations, and operational failures can also sit outside a report's scope.

Before you mint or deposit, review [Risks and parameters](/learn/risks-and-parameters), the [circuit breaker](/security/circuit-breaker), and the selected market's parameters.
