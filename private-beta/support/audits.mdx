## Current status

Nethermind Security completed a security review of the Null V1 contracts (engagement NM-1028). The final report is dated 25 August 2026.

<Card title="Download the final report" icon="file-text" href="https://github.com/NethermindEth/PublicAuditReports/blob/main/NM-1028_Null_Solutions.pdf">
  NM-1028 Null Solutions Security Review (PDF), published in Nethermind's public audit reports repository on GitHub.
</Card>

The review covered 1,220 lines of Solidity in the core and listed periphery: factory, market, pools, settlement, router, imbalance funding-rate model, and Chainlink oracle. Nethermind used manual review and automated analysis.

## Result

The final report recorded five findings and marked all five **Fixed**. There were no Critical or High findings.

| Severity | Count | Status |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Critical | 0 | None |
| High | 0 | None |
| Medium | 1 | Fixed |
| Low | 2 | Fixed |
| Informational | 2 | Fixed |

Nethermind rated the documentation assessment and the test-suite assessment as High.

### Findings

| # | Severity | Finding | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Medium | Shares were calculated before fee recomputation on mint | Fixed |
| 2 | Low | `_deposit` did not revert on zero shares | Fixed |
| 3 | Low | Deposit was cheaper than mint, so a portion of fees was missed | Fixed |
| 4 | Info | Reentrancy-guard implementations were inconsistent across contracts | Fixed |
| 5 | Info | No extra control if a whitelisted account is compromised | Fixed |

## What an audit does not guarantee

A completed audit does not prove that the protocol has no bugs or that a position is safe. Scope and deployment details matter. Oracle behavior, collateral risk, market parameters, integrations, and operational failures can sit outside the report.

Before you mint or deposit, review [Risks and parameters](/learn/risks-and-parameters), the [circuit breaker](/security/circuit-breaker), and the selected market's parameters.
