Use the supplied Null logo files for references to the protocol. The current docs repository includes:

- [Null logo for light backgrounds](/images/Logo.svg)
- [Null logo for dark backgrounds](/images/LogoDark.svg)
- [Null icon mark](/images/LogoIcon.svg)

Choose the full logo when space allows. Use the icon only where the full wordmark would not fit. Keep the artwork's proportions and leave enough surrounding space for it to remain legible.

## Usage requests

The files above are the only downloadable brand assets published in these docs. For other formats, campaign artwork, co-marketing approval, or guidance not covered here, contact the Null team through your private-beta channel. Do not recreate or publish unverified assets as official Null materials.
