Every Null Spot market has two pools:

- **Receiver** follows the tracked asset's price return.
- **Payer** provides the backing for Receiver and takes the opposite side of the price movement.

When you buy, you deposit collateral into your selected pool and receive pool shares. When you sell, you redeem those shares for collateral from that pool.

## Compare the two Spot sides

| Question | Receiver | Payer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What price exposure do I take? | Value rises when the tracked price rises and falls when it falls, subject to settlement limits. | Opposite side: value falls when the tracked price rises and rises when it falls. |
| How large can the price effect be? | Generally follows the tracked move before funding, fees, and other effects. | Payer leverage is `L = R / P`. Lower coverage means higher leverage. |
| How does funding work? | Pays funding equal to a share of collateral APR on Receiver notional (product model: full vault APR). Never receives funding from Payers. | Receives that funding, scaled across the Payer pool (`× R / P` per unit). Also earns collateral yield on its own shares. |
| Do I own the tracked asset? | No. You own ERC-4626 Receiver pool shares. | No. You own ERC-4626 Payer pool shares. |
| What do I receive when I exit? | Your share of Receiver pool collateral after the exit fee. | Your share of Payer pool collateral after the exit fee. |

## Worked price example

Suppose a market has 1,000 collateral units in Receiver and 200 in Payer. The tracked price then rises by 2%.

```text
Price transfer = 2% × 1,000 = 20 units

Receiver: 1,000 → 1,020  (+2%)
Payer:      200 →   180  (-10%)
```

This example excludes funding, fees, collateral yield, and settlement bounds.

## Add funding to the decision

Funding is paid from **collateral APR**, not from a separate signed imbalance rate.

- Receivers pay Payers a share of the vault yield that accrues on Receiver notional.
- Payers do not pay funding back when coverage is high.
- If either pool is empty, settlement applies neither funding nor price movement.

Funding is variable with vault yield. It is not fixed coupon income. See [Funding and carry](/learn/funding-and-carry).

## Margin is different

**Margin** Long / Short positions use Morpho Blue around the same Null pools. They can be liquidated when Morpho LTV reaches LLTV. That is separate from Spot Receiver / Payer pool shares.

See [Morpho](/learn/morpho) and [Using the app](/private-beta/trading/using-the-app).

<Warning>
Spot Receiver and Payer are pool positions, not order-book orders. The Spot UI does not offer a user-selected leverage setting. Margin leverage is a Morpho loop.
</Warning>
