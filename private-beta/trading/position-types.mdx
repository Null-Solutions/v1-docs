Every Null market has two pools:

- **Receiver** — the Receiver side of the market's price and funding exposure.
- **Payer** — the Payer side of the market's price and funding exposure.

Minting deposits collateral into the selected pool and gives you pool shares. Burning redeems your shares for the collateral available from that pool.

Your result can change with the market price, funding, collateral performance, fees, liquidity, and pool state.

<Warning>
Receiver and Payer are pool positions, not conventional order-book orders. The app does not offer a user-selected leverage setting.
</Warning>

Review the market and transaction preview before following [Placing a Position](/private-beta/trading/placing-a-position).
