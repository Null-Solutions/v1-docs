Null markets can charge three protocol fees. Entry and exit fees are based on **notional**, not just the collateral you deposit. For both Receiver and Payer, notional is based on your share of settleable Receiver assets.

<Info>
Check the live transaction preview for your selected market. The collateral fee is currently disabled. When entry or exit fees apply, they usually go to the other pool rather than to Null.
</Info>

| Fee | When it applies | Where to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Entry fee** | When you **Buy** (deposit) pool shares | Trading summary and advanced details |
| **Exit fee** | When you **Sell** (redeem) pool shares | Trading summary and advanced details |
| **Collateral fee** | Disabled. Would accrue over time on both pool balances at settlement | Market details |

Fees can differ by market, so always check the current transaction preview before confirming.

## Notional, not collateral

Entry and exit fee rates are applied to notional:

```text
notional = your assets × settleable Receiver assets / your pool's assets
```

- **Receiver.** When the Receiver pool is above the minimum floor, notional is roughly equal to the collateral you mint or burn. At a 0.25% fee rate, 1,000 units of collateral results in a fee of about 2.49 units.
- **Payer.** Notional scales with short scale `R/P` (negative Receiver size vs Payer capital). At `R/P = 5`, 1,000 units of collateral represents about 5,000 units of notional, so the same 0.25% rate results in a fee of about 12.47 units.

If Receiver is at the minimum floor, or Payer cannot settle when you mint, entry notional is 0 and no entry fee applies. Burns may still have an exit fee.

## Hypothetical mint example

Assume a market shows a **0.25% entry fee** and you mint **1,000 units** of collateral as Receiver. These are example values, not current market settings.

```text
estimated notional   ≈ 1,000
estimated entry fee  ≈ 1,000 × 0.25 / 100.25
                     ≈ 2.49 collateral units

collateral used to mint shares ≈ 997.51 units
```

For Payer, apply the same fee rate to your notional (`assets × R / P`) rather than your collateral amount.

Your pool-share output also depends on the share conversion after settlement. Use the app's estimated output rather than calculating your shares from the fee alone.

## Hypothetical burn example

Assume a market shows a **0.25% exit fee** and a Receiver position has a gross redemption value of **1,000 collateral units**. These are example values.

```text
estimated exit fee ≈ 1,000 × 0.25 / 100.25
                   ≈ 2.49 collateral units

estimated collateral returned ≈ 997.51 units
```

Your final output can change because the market settles before your shares are burned. At a high Payer short scale (`R / P`), the exit fee can be as large as the full redemption amount.

## Collateral fee

The annualized collateral fee is currently disabled. If enabled, it would accrue on pool assets over time at settlement. It would apply to pool assets, not specifically to collateral yield.

Network, onramp, off-ramp, and third-party provider costs are separate from Null's protocol fees.

<Note>
Examples on this page are illustrative. They do not state the live fee for any market. Displayed estimates can change before confirmation as the amount or market state changes.
</Note>

For the protocol-level fee mechanics, see [Null V1 fees](/developers/fees).
