---
title: "Trading Fees"
sidebarTitle: "Fees"
description: "Understand the fees shown when minting or burning a Null position."
---

The trading summary shows the estimated protocol fee for the action you are preparing.

- **Mint** uses the market's entry fee.
- **Burn** uses the market's exit fee.
- A market can also charge an annualized collateral fee as its pools update.

Fee settings can differ by market. Always use the values shown in the current transaction preview.

Wallet, network, onramp, off-ramp, or provider costs are separate from the market's protocol fees.

<Note>
Displayed estimates can change before confirmation as the amount or market state changes.
</Note>

For the protocol-level fee mechanics, see [Null V1 fees](/fees).
