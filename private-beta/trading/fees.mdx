Null markets can charge three protocol fees:

<Info>
During the private beta, the entry fee, exit fee, and annualized collateral fee are all **0%**. You still pay Ethereum gas and any separate wallet, transfer, onramp, or off-ramp costs.
</Info>

| Fee | When it applies | Where to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Entry fee** | When you **Mint** pool shares | Trading summary and **Advanced details** |
| **Exit fee** | When you **Burn** pool shares | Trading summary and **Advanced details** |
| **Collateral fee** | Over time on both pool balances when the market settles | Market details |

Fee settings can differ by market. Always use the values shown in the current transaction preview.

## Hypothetical mint example

Assume a market shows a **0.50% entry fee** and you enter **1,000 units** of collateral. These are example values, not current market settings.

For a collateral amount that includes the fee:

```text
estimated entry fee ≈ 1,000 × 0.50 / 100.50
                    ≈ 4.98 collateral units

collateral used to mint shares ≈ 995.02 units
```

The pool-share output also depends on the pool's share conversion after settlement. Use the app's estimated output instead of calculating shares from the fee alone.

## Hypothetical burn example

Assume a market shows a **0.30% exit fee** and the shares have a gross redemption value of **1,000 collateral units**. These are example values.

```text
estimated exit fee ≈ 1,000 × 0.30 / 100.30
                   ≈ 2.99 collateral units

estimated collateral returned ≈ 997.01 units
```

The confirmed output can change because the market settles before burning the shares.

## Hypothetical collateral-fee example

Assume a market shows a **1.00% annualized collateral fee**. If a pool held a constant 1,000 collateral units for 30 days, a simplified estimate would be:

```text
estimated fee ≈ 1,000 × 1.00% × 30 / 365
              ≈ 0.82 collateral units
```

Actual accrual uses elapsed time and the pool balance at settlement. The collateral fee is charged on pool assets. It is not only a fee on collateral yield.

Wallet, network, onramp, off-ramp, or provider costs are separate from the market's protocol fees.

<Note>
Examples on this page are illustrative. They do not state the live fee for any market. Displayed estimates can change before confirmation as the amount or market state changes.
</Note>

For the protocol-level fee mechanics, see [Null V1 fees](/developers/fees).
