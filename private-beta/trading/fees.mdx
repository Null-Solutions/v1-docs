Null markets can charge three protocol fees. Entry and exit are charged on **notional**, not on collateral alone. Both sides use the same notional: your share of settleable Receiver assets.

<Info>
Check the live transaction preview for the selected market. Collateral fee is disabled. Entry and exit, when set, usually go to the other pool rather than to Null.
</Info>

| Fee | When it applies | Where to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Entry fee** | When you **Mint** pool shares | Trading summary and **Advanced details** |
| **Exit fee** | When you **Burn** pool shares | Trading summary and **Advanced details** |
| **Collateral fee** | Disabled. Would accrue over time on both pool balances at settlement | Market details |

Fee settings can differ by market. Always use the values shown in the current transaction preview.

## Notional, not collateral

The posted rate is basis points on notional:

```text
notional = your assets × settleable Receiver assets / your pool's assets
```

- **Receiver.** When the Receiver pool is well above the minimum floor, notional is about the collateral you mint or burn. A 0.25% rate on 1,000 units is about 2.49 units.
- **Payer.** Notional scales with leverage `R/P`. At 5×, the same 1,000 units of collateral have about 5,000 of notional, so the same 0.25% rate costs about 12.47 units.

If Receiver is at the floor, or you are minting while Payer cannot settle, entry notional is 0 and there is no entry fee. Burns can still charge an exit fee.

## Hypothetical mint example

Assume a market shows a **0.25% entry fee** and you mint **1,000 units** of collateral as Receiver. These are example values, not current market settings.

```text
estimated notional   ≈ 1,000
estimated entry fee  ≈ 1,000 × 0.25 / 100.25
                     ≈ 2.49 collateral units

collateral used to mint shares ≈ 997.51 units
```

On Payer, replace 1,000 with your notional (`assets × R / P`) before applying the same rate.

The pool-share output also depends on the pool's share conversion after settlement. Use the app's estimated output instead of calculating shares from the fee alone.

## Hypothetical burn example

Assume a market shows a **0.25% exit fee** and a Receiver position has a gross redemption value of **1,000 collateral units**. These are example values.

```text
estimated exit fee ≈ 1,000 × 0.25 / 100.25
                   ≈ 2.49 collateral units

estimated collateral returned ≈ 997.51 units
```

The confirmed output can change because the market settles before burning the shares. At high Payer leverage, the exit fee can consume the full redemption.

## Collateral fee

The annualized collateral fee is disabled. If it were enabled, it would accrue on pool assets over time at settlement. It is not a fee on collateral yield.

Wallet, network, onramp, off-ramp, or provider costs are separate from the market's protocol fees.

<Note>
Examples on this page are illustrative. They do not state the live fee for any market. Displayed estimates can change before confirmation as the amount or market state changes.
</Note>

For the protocol-level fee mechanics, see [Null V1 fees](/developers/fees).
