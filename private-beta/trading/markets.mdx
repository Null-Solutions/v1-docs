Open `/markets` to browse available markets. Select a row to open its market page.

## Review before trading

Depending on available data, the market list shows:

- the market pair and oracle;
- current price and recent price change;
- market size;
- collateral and funding rates;
- Receiver or Payer market information;
- collateral and fee information; and
- whether the market is online, closed, or missing Payer funds.

The market page adds charts, pool information, transaction details, and risk information.

<Note>
A visible market is not always available for a transaction. Check the current market status and price data in the trading panel.
</Note>
