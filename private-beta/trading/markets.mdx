Open **`/trade`** to browse Spot markets. Select a row to open a market slide-out. Portfolio Spot lives on Home under **Spot**; leveraged markets use **Home → Margin**.

## Market list fields

The list can show the following fields when data is available:

| Field | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Market** | The tracked asset. Subtitle may identify the oracle source. |
| **Last 7 days** | Recent reference-price chart. Not an order book. |
| **Market price** | Latest oracle reference price. It can differ from the last settled contract price until the next update. |
| **7d change** | Percentage change across the displayed seven-day series. |
| **Market size** | Combined collateral value of Receiver and Payer pools. Not trading volume. |
| **Collateral APR** | Estimated annualized yield of the collateral vault. Both pools accrue it; Receivers pay that yield (or a configured share) to Payers as funding. |
| **Funding APR** | Annualized funding paid by Receivers to Payers from collateral yield. In the current product model it tracks Collateral APR and is never negative. |
| **Net APR** | Estimated carry for a new position after combining collateral yield and funding. |
| **Collateral** | Token deposited into both pools (often a Morpho vault share such as `nmUSDC`). |

Charts on a market can include price, funding APR, and collateral APR when history is indexed.

## Pool coverage (not order-book depth)

Null Spot does not use an order book. Capacity comes from collateral already in the two pools.

```text
Payer coverage = Payer pool assets / Receiver pool assets
Payer leverage L = R / P
```

- **Market size** is both pools combined.
- **Receiver liquidity** is collateral on the Receiver side.
- **Payer liquidity** is collateral backing that side.
- Lower coverage raises Payer leverage, so the same price transfer hits Payer harder in percentage terms.

Funding no longer flips with coverage the way a signed imbalance curve would. Coverage still matters for Payer leverage and for how funding on Receiver notional spreads across the Payer pool (`f × R / P` per unit of Payer).

<Warning>
A visible market is not always tradable. **Closed** markets cannot be traded. **No Payer funds** blocks Receiver operation until the Payer pool has assets. Missing or invalid oracle data can also block a transaction. Margin requires a separate Morpho long/short config; see [Morpho](/learn/morpho).
</Warning>

Displayed APRs and previews are **not** guaranteed execution values. The market settles before a Spot trade converts collateral and shares.
