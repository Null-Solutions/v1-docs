Open `/markets` to browse available markets. Select a row to open its market page.

## Market list fields

The list can show the following fields when data is available:

| Field | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Market** | The tracked asset and quote pair. The subtitle identifies the oracle source used when the market settles. |
| **Last 7 days** | A chart of recent reference-price observations. It does not show executable orders. |
| **Market price** | The latest available oracle reference price. This may differ from the price currently stored by the contract until its next update. |
| **7d change** | The percentage change across the displayed seven-day price series. |
| **Market size** | The current collateral value of the Receiver and Payer pools combined. It is not trading volume or order-book depth. |
| **Collateral APR** | The estimated annualized yield of the collateral asset. It is separate from funding and can change. |
| **Funding APR** | The signed annualized rate produced by the market's funding-rate model. Positive means Receiver pays Payer. Negative means Payer pays Receiver. |
| **Payer Bias** | A comparison of Receiver and Payer pool sizes shown in the Payer view. Check the underlying pool balances to understand how concentrated the Payer side is. |
| **Collateral** | The token deposited into both pools and returned when shares are burned. It is not necessarily the tracked asset. |

The market page adds contract price, Receiver and Payer liquidity, fees, pool and contract addresses, charts, transaction history, and risk information.

## Understanding market depth through pool coverage

Null does not use an order book, so there are no bids, asks, or quoted depth levels. Instead, market capacity comes from the collateral already held in the two pools.

Compare:

```text
Payer coverage = Payer pool assets / Receiver pool assets
```

- **Market size** tells you how much collateral both pools hold in total.
- **Receiver liquidity** tells you how much collateral is currently on the Receiver side.
- **Payer liquidity** tells you how much collateral is currently backing the Receiver side.
- **Payer coverage** shows how much Payer collateral backs each unit of Receiver collateral.

A large market can still have low Payer coverage. Payer leverage is `L = R / P`. As coverage falls, leverage rises, so the same transfer has a larger percentage impact on Payer share value.

Funding changes with coverage too. For the standard funding-rate model, funding is zero at 100% Payer coverage (`P = R`). Another enabled model can use a different target. Check the current signed funding rate and the market's funding-rate model before choosing a side.

<Warning>
A visible market is not always available for a transaction. **Closed** markets cannot be traded. **No Payer funds** means Receiver positions cannot operate until there are assets in the Payer pool. Missing or invalid oracle data can also block a transaction.
</Warning>

Displayed APRs, pool balances, and previews are **not** guaranteed execution values. The market settles before a transaction converts collateral and shares, so funding, price movement, fees, or collateral changes can affect the final result.
