The Null app is organized around **Home** and a few dedicated routes. Home uses section tabs so one account can show Savings, Spot, Lending, and Margin without mixing them into a dashboard of unrelated cards.

## Home sections

Open `/` and use the section pills (desktop) or section menu (mobile):

| Section | URL | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | `/` | Portfolio summary, chart, and product groups: Savings, Spot, Lending, Margin, plus Cash |
| **Savings** | `/?section=savings` | Null Savings Vault balance, add / withdraw, allocation context |
| **Spot** | `/?section=spot` | Spot positions (Receiver / Payer pool shares), markets rail |
| **Lending** | `/?section=lending` | Morpho lending positions and a path to the full lending book |
| **Margin** | `/?section=margin` | Full **Margin desk** for Morpho long / short loops |

**Cash** is the USDC in your connected wallet. There is no separate custodial cash balance.

From Overview, empty states read as "No savings yet", "No spot yet", "No margin yet". Savings rows open as **Null Savings Vault**. Margin rows show **Long** / **Short**.

## Savings

Savings deposits USDC into the **Null Savings Vault** (Morpho Vault V2 `nmUSDC`). Slide-outs cover product info, **Add money**, and **Withdraw**. Interest accrues in share value. The displayed rate is the Morpho vault supply AER.

Details: [Vaults](/learn/vaults).

## Spot and the trade terminal

Spot is unlevered Null pool exposure.

- Browse markets at `/trade` (legacy `/markets` redirects here).
- Open a market to get a **slide-out** stack: market detail, then a trade panel for amount → review → progress.
- Primary actions are **Buy** / **Sell** on the Receiver side. Pro mode can trade Payer as well.
- Deep links look like `/trade/[address]?action=buy|sell&side=receiver|payer`.

Charts can show price, funding APR, and collateral APR history when the indexer provides series.

Market list fields and APR definitions: [Markets](/private-beta/trading/markets).

## Lending

Lending supplies assets into Morpho Blue markets that sit around Null:

- USDC into **long** markets (loan USDC, collateral = receiver shares)
- Receiver shares into **short** markets (loan shares, collateral = `nmUSDC`)

Home Lending summarizes your positions. `/lending` is the full book (`?id=` focuses a row). Pair labels look like `AAPL | nmUSDC` when a share is lent against the vault.

## Margin desk

Margin is only on **Home → Margin** (not on `/trade`). The desk includes:

- Market picker for Null markets that have loop / short config
- **Long** / **Short** direction tabs
- Tickets (`LoopTerminal` / `ShortTerminal`) for open and close
- Charts: price, pools, borrow rate, collateral APR
- **My Positions** and **My Activity**
- Stats including long/short Morpho market ids and borrow APRs

Empty copy appears when a market cannot be margined yet or has no long/short book.

Mechanics: [Morpho](/learn/morpho).

## Points (Season 0)

`/points` shows **Season 0 has started**. Normal trading activity is monitored and counts toward points on that page. The detailed rewards UI may expand later; referrals currently redirect to `/points`.

See [Points and referrals](/private-beta/status-and-points/points-and-referrals).
