In the Spot UI, **Buy** opens a Receiver or Payer position and **Sell** exits it. Onchain those are ERC-4626 deposit and redeem (router `depositMarket` / `redeemMarket`).

Before submitting, review the selected market's [fees](/private-beta/trading/fees). Use the current preview for live values. Examples in the docs are illustrative only.

## Buy a Spot position

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a market">
    Open `/trade` and select a market. Check its status, oracle price, collateral, Receiver and Payer liquidity, collateral APR, funding APR, and fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Receiver or Payer">
    Choose the side that matches the price exposure you want. Read [Position types](/private-beta/trading/position-types) before continuing. Primary UI actions target Receiver; Pro mode can trade Payer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Buy and enter amount">
    Choose **Buy** and enter the amount to deposit. If your wallet does not hold enough Cash / collateral, fund it before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the preview">
    Check your selected side, estimated pool shares, fees, and market status. Expand advanced details when available to verify market, pool, network, and price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve if prompted">
    If prompted, approve the router to spend the required token. Approval alone does not open the position.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Confirm in your wallet. The market settles first, then collateral is deposited and pool shares are minted. Wait for onchain confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sell a Spot position

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your position">
    Open the same market from `/trade` or Home → Spot. Connect the wallet that holds the pool shares.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Sell">
    Choose **Sell** for the side you hold and enter the share amount (or max).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and submit">
    Check the preview, confirm in your wallet, and wait for settlement plus redemption to complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the transaction

- Spot positions appear under Home → Spot and in the market panel.
- Funding and price continue to settle on later interactions.
- For leveraged exposure, use Home → Margin instead; see [Morpho](/learn/morpho).

<Warning>
Previews are not guarantees. Settlement, fees, oracle updates, and pool state can change the final output.
</Warning>
