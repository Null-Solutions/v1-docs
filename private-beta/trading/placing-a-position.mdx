---

Null's trading page uses **Mint** and **Burn** for Receiver and Payer pool positions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a market">
    Open `/markets`, review the available markets, and select one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Receiver or Payer">
    Select the side that matches the exposure you want. Read [Position Types](/private-beta/trading/position-types) before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Mint or Burn">
    **Mint** deposits market collateral and creates pool shares. **Burn** redeems shares from an existing position.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    Check your balance and the estimated output. The app may ask you to fund your wallet first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the summary">
    Check the selected side, current and next position, share balance, fee, and market status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Approve any required wallet request, submit the transaction, and wait for on-chain confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Null does not currently present an order book, limit orders, stop orders, or a configurable leverage control. Do not assume those behaviors apply.
</Warning>
