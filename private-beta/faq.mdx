## What Null is

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Null?">
    Null lets you hold onchain price exposure to assets while your collateral can continue earning DeFi yield. Spot is synthetic exposure through pooled Receiver and Payer shares. The app also includes Savings, Lending, and Morpho Margin.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is this different from a perps DEX?">
    Perps are built for leveraged trading with margin accounts. Null Spot is built for holding price exposure in fully collateralized pools: no account leverage to configure, no margin calls on Spot, no order book. If you want leveraged Null exposure, use **Margin** (Morpho loops) instead of Spot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does Spot work?">
    Every market has two pools on the same price feed. Receivers deposit for price exposure. Payers provide backing. Funding moves vault yield from Receivers to Payers. See [The primitive](/private-beta/about-null/the-primitive).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do I actually hold?">
    Spot positions are ERC-4626 vault shares for a Receiver or Payer pool. Savings holds Morpho vault shares (`nmUSDC`). Margin holds a Morpho Blue position around those pools. See [Position types](/private-beta/trading/position-types) and [Using the app](/private-beta/trading/using-the-app).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I own the underlying asset?">
    No. Null gives you synthetic exposure. Your Spot position tracks the reference price through collateralized pools, but the referenced asset itself is not held for you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Yield, funding, and vaults

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where does the yield come from?">
    Yield comes from the Morpho vault used as market collateral and as the Null Savings Vault. Borrowers on Morpho Blue pay interest; that supply rate is Collateral APR / Savings AER. Spot funding pays Receiver-side vault yield to Payers. See [Vaults](/learn/vaults) and [Funding and carry](/learn/funding-and-carry).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is funding a separate rate?">
    No. There is no separate floating funding rate against an external index. Funding is the collateral APR (or a fixed share of it) paid by Receivers to Payers. Payers do not pay funding the other way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the Null Savings Vault?">
    Savings is USDC deposited into a Morpho Vault V2 (`nmUSDC`). That vault allocates USDC to Morpho Blue **long** markets that lend against Null receiver shares. It is the same vault share Null markets use as collateral on the Morpho loop stack.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why would anyone take the Payer side?">
    Payers earn funding from Receiver-side vault yield and can hedge price exposure elsewhere. See [Market making](/private-beta/market-make/overview).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Using Null

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I trade Spot?">
    Open `/trade` for the markets list and slide-out trade terminal. Home → Spot shows your Spot positions. See [Using the app](/private-beta/trading/using-the-app).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is Margin?">
    Margin opens Morpho long or short loops on configured Null markets via `NullV1MorphoRouter`. Morpho can liquidate at LLTV. See [Morpho](/learn/morpho).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I exit Spot?">
    Sell (redeem) your pool shares. The market settles first, so the confirmed output can differ from the preview.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are the fees?">
    Markets can charge entry and exit fees on notional. An annualized collateral fee exists but is disabled. Check the live preview. See [Trading fees](/private-beta/trading/fees).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to complete KYC?">
    There is no identity-document verification in the documented flow. Connect your own wallet, or sign in with a supported login. Eligibility can still be restricted by jurisdiction. See [Get started](/private-beta/getting-started/null-private-beta).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Risk

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I be liquidated?">
    Spot Receiver and Payer positions do not have user-selected leverage or individual Null liquidations. **Margin** Morpho positions can be liquidated when LTV reaches LLTV. See [Morpho](/learn/morpho) and [Risks](/private-beta/getting-started/before-you-trade).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are the risks?">
    Risks include position losses, smart-contract risk, oracle risk, collateral and vault risk, Morpho liquidation (Margin), tracking error, liquidity, and pool solvency. Read the risk pages before depositing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Has Null been audited?">
    Nethermind Security completed a review of the Null V1 contracts (final report August 2026): five findings, all fixed, none critical or high. An audit does not guarantee safety. See [Audits](/private-beta/support/audits).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Points

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Season 0?">
    Season 0 has started. Normal trading activity is monitored and counts toward points on `/points`. Points are not money, yield, or a promise of any token or payout. See [Points and referrals](/private-beta/status-and-points/points-and-referrals).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
