Private-beta access is approved per effective Ethereum wallet. The app supports email, social, passkey, and external-wallet sign-in; it creates an embedded wallet for accounts that do not already have one. Complete the application with the same account you plan to use in the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the app's `/beta` route and select **Sign in**. The effective Ethereum wallet associated with that account is shown during agreement signing and becomes the address associated with your application.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Provide your details">
    Enter your full name and email address. If you are applying for an entity, also provide the entity name and your title.

    You can optionally add a referral code and social handles. A referral code is not required to apply.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign the Beta Participation Agreement">
    Review and complete the Beta Participation Agreement in the embedded DocuSeal form. Confirm that the wallet shown above the form is the address you intend to use.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    After signing, the app marks your application as received. An administrator must still approve your wallet on-chain before you can access private-beta routes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the beta">
    Return with the approved wallet and sign in. Once its on-chain approval is available, the app opens the private-beta experience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Signing the agreement submits an application; it does not grant immediate access. Keep using the same sign-in account while your application is pending.
</Info>

## After signing

The completed screen shows your wallet and any available application details. It offers a download when a copy of the signed agreement is available. If the app still returns you to `/beta`, your wallet has not yet received access.

Continue with [Fund Account](/private-beta/fund-account) after approval.
