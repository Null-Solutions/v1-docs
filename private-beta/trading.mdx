The app separates market discovery from trading:

- `/markets` lists available markets.
- `/markets/[address]` opens the trading view for one market.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse markets">
    Open `/markets` and compare the available markets. Select a row to open that market's address-specific page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the market">
    Check the market pair, oracle information, pool data, rates, fees, and risk details before acting.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose an action and side">
    Use **Mint** to deposit into a Receiver or Payer pool and receive shares. Use **Burn** to redeem shares from an existing position.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Check the amount and transaction preview, then approve and confirm through your wallet. Wait for on-chain confirmation before treating the action as complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>
