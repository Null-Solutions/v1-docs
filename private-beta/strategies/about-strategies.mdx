Managed strategies are not currently available in the private-beta app.

There is no strategy marketplace, allocation flow, manager selection, or automated strategy configuration to use at this time.

<Info>
This page documents availability only. It does not announce a launch date or promise future strategy features.
</Info>

You can still choose individual markets and manage Receiver or Payer positions directly. Start with [Markets](/private-beta/trading/markets).
