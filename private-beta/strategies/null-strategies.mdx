Null managed strategies are not currently available.

You cannot deposit into, withdraw from, compare, or configure a managed strategy in the private-beta app.

Until this changes:

1. Browse available [Markets](/private-beta/trading/markets).
2. Review [Position Types](/private-beta/trading/position-types).
3. Manage each position directly from its market page.

<Warning>
Do not send funds to an address presented as a Null managed strategy unless that product is available in the official app and documented here.
</Warning>
