Null lets you hold **ERC-20 tokens designed to track the price of stocks and shares** in your wallet.

You deposit collateral, mint, and get a token that tracks the stock. You do not buy the real share. You do not borrow it.

On the other side are **Market Makers**. They provide the backing that makes Receiver positions possible and earn **funding** for doing it.

## Two sides

Every Null market has two sides: **Receivers**, who want price exposure, and **Payers**, who provide the backing for it.

### Receiver: get price exposure

Choose **Receiver** if you want exposure to the asset's price through an ERC-20 token.

- You get an ERC-20 designed to track the price of the underlying stock or share.
- If the asset's price goes up, the token's value goes up. If it goes down, the token's value goes down.
- There is no leverage setting and no liquidation of your Receiver account.
- Burn the token when you want your collateral back.

<Info>
  This is not the real stock. You do not get voting rights or dividends unless
  the market's price already includes them. You can still lose money.
</Info>

### Payer: provide backing and earn funding

Choose **Payer** if you want to provide backing for Receiver tokens and earn funding.

Receivers need someone on the other side. Payers provide collateral to back the Receiver's tokens. If the stock goes up, Payers lose. If it goes down, they gain.

You get paid **funding** for providing that backing, especially when there is not enough of it. Many Payers also buy the underlying real stock elsewhere to help offset price risk. What remains is funding, plus any yield on the collateral, minus the cost of the hedge.

Funding can flip sign. The hedge can cost more than it returns. During the private beta, Payers manage this strategy themselves. Null does not offer a one-click hedging strategy.

## How the app works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse markets">
    Pick a stock or share. Check the price, collateral, pool sizes, funding, and fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Receiver or Payer">
    Receiver: get price exposure. Payer: provide backing and earn funding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mint">
    Deposit collateral. Receiver gets the stock token. Payer gets the backing token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the position">
    Price, funding, and collateral can all move the value. Payers also watch their hedge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Burn to exit">
    Burn the token to get collateral back from that pool.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What sits in your wallet

The ERC-20 sits in your wallet. The collateral sits in the market contracts.

A Receiver token is designed to track the price of the underlying stock or share. It is not the underlying asset. Its value can also be affected by funding, fees, and the collateral used by the market.

## Private beta access

The private beta is invitation-only and runs on Ethereum mainnet. It is a limited technical test, not a public launch, and may end at any time.

Approved participants can:

- [apply with a wallet](/private-beta/getting-started/null-private-beta) and complete the participation agreement;
- [fund that wallet](/private-beta/getting-started/fund-your-account) with supported collateral and ETH for gas;
- [mint or burn a position](/private-beta/trading/placing-a-position);
- [operate a hedged Payer position](/private-beta/market-make/overview); and
- view [Points and referrals](/private-beta/status-and-points/points-and-referrals).

<Warning>
  You can lose some or all of the assets you put in. Check the collateral in
  the app against the [addresses page](/resources/addresses) before you transact.
</Warning>
