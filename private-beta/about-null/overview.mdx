Null lets you hold stocks and shares as **ERC-20 tokens** in your wallet.

You deposit collateral, mint, and get a token that tracks the stock. You do not buy the real share. You do not borrow it.

Someone has to back those tokens. That is the **Payer**. Payers market-make the inventory and earn **funding** for doing it.

## Two sides

Every market has a Receiver side and a Payer side.

### Receiver: hold the stock token

Mint **Receiver** if you want the token.

- You get an ERC-20 that tracks the stock or share.
- If the price goes up, the token is worth more. If it goes down, it is worth less.
- There is no leverage setting and no liquidation of your Receiver account.
- Burn the token when you want your collateral back.

<Info>
  This is not the real stock. You do not get voting rights or dividends unless
  the market's price already includes them. You can still lose money.
</Info>

### Payer: market-make it for funding

Mint **Payer** if you want to back those tokens and earn funding.

Receivers need someone on the other side. You put up collateral to back their tokens. You lose when the stock goes up and gain when it goes down.

You get paid **funding** for providing that backing, especially when there is not enough of it. Many Payers also buy the real stock elsewhere so the price risk cancels out. What is left is funding, plus any yield on the collateral, minus the cost of the hedge.

Funding can flip sign. The hedge can cost more than it returns. In the private beta you run this yourself. Null does not offer a one-click strategy.

## How the app works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse markets">
    Pick a stock or share. Check the price, collateral, pool sizes, funding, and fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Receiver or Payer">
    Receiver: hold the token. Payer: market-make it for funding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mint">
    Deposit collateral. Receiver gets the stock token. Payer gets the backing token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the position">
    Price, funding, and collateral can all move the value. Payers also watch their hedge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Burn to exit">
    Burn the token to get collateral back from that pool.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you own

The ERC-20 sits in your wallet. The collateral sits in the market contracts.

A Receiver token tracks the stock. It is not the stock. Its value can change with price, funding, fees, and the collateral itself.

## Private beta access

The private beta is invitation-only on Ethereum mainnet. It is a short technical test. It is not a launch, and it may end at any time.

Approved participants can:

- [apply with a wallet](/private-beta/getting-started/null-private-beta) and complete the participation agreement;
- [fund that wallet](/private-beta/getting-started/fund-your-account) with supported collateral and ETH for gas;
- [mint or burn a position](/private-beta/trading/placing-a-position);
- [operate a hedged Payer position](/private-beta/market-make/overview); and
- view [Points and referrals](/private-beta/status-and-points/points-and-referrals).

<Warning>
  You can lose some or all of the assets you put in. Check the collateral in
  the app against the [addresses page](/resources/addresses) before you transact.
</Warning>
