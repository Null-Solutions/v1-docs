You mint an **ERC-20 token** that tracks a stock, share, or other listed return. Deposit the market's collateral, and the token lands in your wallet. You do not buy or borrow the real asset.

**Payers market-make those tokens.** They put up the backing so Receivers can exist, take the other side of the price, and earn funding for doing so.

A market can reference a stock, share, cryptoasset, index, or another return an oracle can report. Collateral can be yield-bearing, so that yield can keep accruing in the pool while the position is open.

## Choose how you participate

Every market has two sides: **Receiver** and **Payer**.

### Hold the token

Choose **Receiver** to get the ERC-20 that tracks the stock or share. It generally gains when the accepted oracle price rises and loses when it falls.

That is meant to feel closer to holding the asset than running a margin account:

- you mint a token, you do not borrow the stock;
- you do not choose leverage; and
- the protocol does not liquidate individual Receiver accounts.

Pick a market, check its collateral and oracle, mint the Receiver token, and burn it when you want your collateral back.

<Info>
  This is synthetic exposure, not ownership of the listed stock or share. You
  do not get voting rights, dividends, or custody of the real asset unless the
  market's oracle return already includes them. Receiver can lose most or all
  of its value through adverse price moves, funding, collateral behavior,
  oracle behavior, liquidity, or contract failures.
</Info>

### Market-make it for funding

Choose **Payer** to market-make the Receiver token. You supply the collateral that backs those tokens and take the opposite price transfer: you generally lose when the tracked price rises and gain when it falls.

The reason to do that is funding. When Receiver demand needs more backing, Payers earn funding for providing it. A market maker can hedge the short by holding an external long in the stock or a close equivalent, and aim to keep:

- funding paid by Receiver when Payer backing is scarce;
- yield on the deposited collateral; and
- any difference between Null funding and the cost of the external hedge.

Funding can reverse, collateral yield can change, and the hedge can underperform. Market making is self-operated during the private beta. Null does not currently provide a one-click managed strategy.

## How the app works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse markets">
    Compare the tracked stock or share, oracle, supported collateral, pool sizes, Payer
    coverage, current funding, and fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Receiver or Payer">
    Select Receiver to mint the ERC-20 token. Select Payer to market-make it for
    funding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mint with collateral">
    Deposit the market's supported collateral. Receiver mints the ERC-20 that
    tracks the stock or share. Payer mints the ERC-20 that backs it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor the position">
    Track price PnL, funding, collateral performance, pool coverage, and the
    value represented by each token. Market makers must also monitor and
    rebalance their external hedge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Burn to exit">
    Burn your tokens to redeem your proportional claim on the collateral
    then available in that pool.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you own

You control the wallet that holds your Receiver or Payer ERC-20 tokens. While a position is open, its collateral sits in the market's smart contracts. A Receiver token tracks the listed return. It is not the listed stock or share itself.

Token value can change through price PnL, funding, fees, collateral performance, and settlement. If the collateral is yield-bearing, its yield contributes to pool value but is not fixed or guaranteed.

## Private beta access

The private beta is invitation-only on Ethereum mainnet. It is a time-limited technical test, not a permanent product, public launch, or roadmap commitment. Null may change, restrict, suspend, or end access at any time.

Approved participants can:

- [apply with a wallet](/private-beta/getting-started/null-private-beta) and complete the participation agreement;
- [fund that wallet](/private-beta/getting-started/fund-your-account) with supported collateral and ETH for gas;
- [mint or burn a position](/private-beta/trading/placing-a-position);
- [operate a hedged Payer position](/private-beta/market-make/overview); and
- view [Points and referrals](/private-beta/status-and-points/points-and-referrals).

<Warning>
  Positions can lose some or all of their value through price moves, funding,
  fees, collateral behavior, oracle behavior, liquidity, or contract failures.
  Verify supported collateral in the app and its contract address on the
  canonical [addresses page](/resources/addresses) before transacting.
</Warning>
