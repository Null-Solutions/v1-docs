Null's private beta gives approved wallets access to funding, markets, trading, portfolios, and referrals.

During the private beta, you can:

- [apply for access](/private-beta/getting-started/null-private-beta) and complete the participation agreement;
- [fund your account](/private-beta/getting-started/fund-your-account) with crypto, cash, or testnet assets when available;
- [mint or burn a position](/private-beta/trading/placing-a-position) in a Receiver or Payer pool; and
- [review your portfolio](/private-beta/portfolios/portfolio-overview), balances, history, and saved markets.

## Not available

Managed strategies and a points economy are not currently available. The referral number shown in the app is a successful-referral count.

<Warning>
Private-beta access does not remove market, oracle, liquidity, or smart-contract risk. Only use funds you can afford to lose.
</Warning>
