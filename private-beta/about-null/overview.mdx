Null is a platform for creating and trading collateralized synthetic exposure. A market can reference a real-world asset, cryptoasset, index, or another measurable return supported by an oracle.

You do not need to buy or borrow the tracked asset. Instead, you mint a position with the market's supported collateral. That collateral can be a yield-bearing asset, allowing its underlying yield to continue contributing to the pool while the position is open.

Null brings together two types of participant:

- people who want simple, spot-like synthetic exposure to a tracked return; and
- market makers who supply the backing for that exposure and manage it as a carry strategy.

## Choose how you participate

Every market has two sides: **Receiver** and **Payer**.

### Get synthetic exposure

Choose **Receiver** when you want the tracked return. Receiver generally gains when the accepted oracle price rises and loses when it falls.

The experience is designed to feel closer to holding a spot position than managing a margin account:

- you do not borrow the tracked asset;
- you do not choose leverage; and
- the protocol does not liquidate individual Receiver accounts.

You choose a market, review its collateral and oracle, mint Receiver shares, monitor the position, and burn those shares when you want to exit.

<Info>
  Spot-like does not mean capital-protected or low-risk. Receiver can lose most
  or all of its value through adverse price moves, funding, collateral behavior,
  oracle behavior, liquidity, or contract failures.
</Info>

### Market make for carry

Choose **Payer** when you want to supply the collateral reserve that backs Receiver. Payer takes the opposite price transfer: it generally loses when the tracked price rises and gains when it falls.

A market maker can reduce that directional exposure by combining Payer with an external long in the tracked asset or a close equivalent. The objective is to earn net carry from:

- funding paid by Receiver when Payer backing is scarce;
- yield generated by the deposited collateral; and
- any difference between Null funding and the cost of the external hedge.

Funding can reverse, collateral yield can change, and the hedge can underperform. Market making is self-operated during the private beta. Null does not currently provide a one-click managed strategy.

## How the app works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse markets">
    Compare the tracked return, oracle, supported collateral, pool sizes, Payer
    coverage, current funding, and fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Receiver or Payer">
    Select Receiver for the tracked return. Select Payer to supply backing and
    take the other side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mint with collateral">
    Deposit the market's supported collateral and receive transferable ERC-4626
    shares in the selected pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor the position">
    Track price PnL, funding, collateral performance, pool coverage, and the
    value represented by each share. Market makers must also monitor and
    rebalance their external hedge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Burn to exit">
    Burn your pool shares to redeem your proportional claim on the collateral
    then available in that pool.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you own

You control the wallet that holds your Receiver or Payer shares. While a position is open, its collateral is deposited in the market's smart contracts. You own pool shares representing a proportional claim on that collateral, not the tracked asset itself.

Receiver and Payer share value can change through price PnL, funding, fees, collateral performance, and settlement. If the collateral is yield-bearing, its yield contributes to pool value but is not fixed or guaranteed.

## Private beta access

The private beta is invitation-only on Ethereum mainnet. Approved participants can:

- [apply with a wallet](/private-beta/getting-started/null-private-beta) and complete the participation agreement;
- [fund that wallet](/private-beta/getting-started/fund-your-account) with supported collateral and ETH for gas;
- [mint or burn a position](/private-beta/trading/placing-a-position);
- [operate a hedged Payer position](/private-beta/market-make/overview); and
- view [Points and referrals](/private-beta/status-and-points/points-and-referrals).

<Warning>
  Positions can lose some or all of their value through price moves, funding,
  fees, collateral behavior, oracle behavior, liquidity, or contract failures.
  Verify supported collateral in the app and its contract address on the
  canonical [addresses page](/resources/addresses) before transacting.
</Warning>
