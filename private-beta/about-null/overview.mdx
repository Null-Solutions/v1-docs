Null lets you hold **ERC-20 tokens designed to track the price of stocks and shares** in your wallet.

You deposit collateral, buy a pool share, and get a token that tracks the stock. You do not buy the real share. You do not borrow it for Spot. The token sits in yield-bearing collateral, and **funding** moves a share of that vault yield from Receivers to the market makers who back them.

## Two sides (Spot)

Every Null market has two sides: **Receivers**, who want price exposure, and **Payers**, who provide the backing.

### Receiver: get price exposure

Choose **Receiver** if you want exposure to the asset's price through an ERC-20 token.

- You get an ERC-20 designed to track the price of the underlying stock or share.
- If the asset's price goes up, the token's value goes up. If it goes down, the token's value goes down.
- Spot Receiver has no leverage setting and no Null-account liquidation.
- Sell (burn) the token when you want your collateral back.

<Info>
  This is not the real stock. You do not get voting rights or dividends unless
  the market's price already includes them. You can still lose money.
</Info>

### Payer: provide backing and earn funding

Choose **Payer** if you want to provide backing for Receiver tokens and earn funding.

Receivers need someone on the other side. Payers provide collateral to back Receiver tokens. If the stock goes up, Payers lose. If it goes down, they gain.

**Funding is paid from collateral APR.** Receivers pay vault yield on the Receiver pool to Payers. Payers do not pay funding the other way. Many Payers also buy the underlying elsewhere to offset price risk. What remains is funding, plus yield on their own collateral, minus hedge costs.

Funding yield is variable. The hedge can cost more than it returns. Null does not offer a one-click hedging strategy; market makers manage hedges themselves. See [Market making](/private-beta/market-make/overview).

## What the app includes

Beyond Spot Receiver / Payer, the app has:

| Area | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Savings** | Deposit USDC into the **Null Savings Vault** (Morpho). Earn Morpho supply yield from long-market lending. |
| **Spot** | Unlevered Null pool shares. Trade from `/trade` slide-outs. |
| **Lending** | Supply USDC or receiver shares into Morpho books around Null. |
| **Margin** | Morpho **Long** / **Short** loops via `NullV1MorphoRouter` (LLTV liquidations apply on Morpho). |
| **Points** | Season 0 monitors normal trading activity toward points. |

Walkthrough: [Using the app](/private-beta/trading/using-the-app). Vault roles: [Vaults](/learn/vaults). Morpho loops: [Morpho](/learn/morpho).

## How Spot works in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse markets">
    Open `/trade`. Check price, collateral APR, funding APR, pools, and fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Receiver or Payer">
    Receiver: price exposure. Payer: backing and funding from vault yield.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy">
    Deposit collateral. Receiver gets the stock token. Payer gets the backing token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the position">
    Price, funding, and collateral can all move value. Payers also watch any external hedge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sell to exit">
    Redeem the token for collateral from that pool.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What sits in your wallet

The ERC-20 sits in your wallet. The collateral sits in the market contracts (or in Morpho when you use Margin / Lending).

A Receiver token is designed to track the underlying price. Its value can also move with funding, fees, and the collateral vault.

<Warning>
  You can lose some or all of the assets you put in. Check the collateral in
  the app against the [addresses page](/resources/addresses) before you transact.
</Warning>
