Null is a pooled, fully collateralized total-return-swap primitive. It creates onchain exposure to the return of an oracle-tracked asset without borrowing, liquidations, or a bilateral counterparty.

## Two sides

Every market has two collateral pools:

- **Receiver** takes the tracked asset's price return.
- **Payer** supplies the reserve collateral backing Receiver gains and takes the opposite price exposure.

Users deposit collateral into one side and receive fungible ERC-4626 pool shares. A share represents a proportional claim on that pool rather than a position with a private entry price.

## Immutable market rules

Each market is defined by three contracts:

```text
(collateral, oracle, funding-rate model)
```

The collateral token, oracle, and funding-rate model are fixed when the market is created. The oracle supplies the reference price, while the funding-rate model adjusts payments between Receiver and Payer according to pool coverage.

## How settlement works

Before a position changes, the market:

1. validates the latest oracle price;
2. reconciles the collateral held by the pools;
3. accrues collateral fees and signed funding;
4. calculates price profit and loss; and
5. settles the net transfer between Receiver and Payer.

All gains come from collateral already held by the market. Settlement cannot create debt or an undercollateralized claim, and its transfer is bounded to preserve the protocol's minimum-pool floor.

## What this means for users

Receiver and Payer are pooled positions, not leveraged order-book trades. Their value can change with price returns, funding, collateral performance, fees, liquidity, and the state of both pools.

<Warning>
Full collateralization limits the primitive to assets already held by the market. It does not remove oracle, smart-contract, liquidity, collateral, or market risk.
</Warning>
