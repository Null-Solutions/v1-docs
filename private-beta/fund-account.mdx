---

On Null, funding means depositing supported collateral into a specific market. It is not a bank transfer, wire, or deposit into a custodial Null account.

The transaction moves collateral from your wallet into the market's smart contracts and mints pool shares to your wallet. Null does not hold the collateral in a custodial account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a market">
    Go to `/markets`, select a market, and open its page at `/markets/[address]`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a side">
    Select the Receiver or Payer side. Each side has a separate pool and risk profile.

    Read [Learn Null V1](/learn/overview) before choosing if you are unfamiliar with the Receiver/Payer model.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    Use collateral supported by that market. Review the preview, fees, expected shares, and transaction conditions shown by your wallet and the app.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve and confirm">
    If required, approve the market flow to use the collateral, then confirm the deposit transaction in your wallet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify the position">
    After confirmation, the position and pool-share balance appear in your signed-in portfolio.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
A market deposit exposes your collateral to the selected pool's price, funding, oracle, liquidity, and smart-contract risks. It is not a cash balance.
</Warning>

For deposit mechanics, approvals, previews, slippage protection, and deadlines, read [Depositing](/depositing). See [Fees](/fees) for protocol fee behavior.
