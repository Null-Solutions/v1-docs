## Application status

Your private-beta application can be understood in three stages:

1. **Not submitted** — complete your details and the Beta Participation Agreement at `/beta`.
2. **Application received** — your agreement is signed, but your wallet is still waiting for approval.
3. **Access approved** — your wallet has been approved on-chain and can enter protected private-beta routes.

<Info>
Agreement completion and beta access are separate. Signing records your application; an administrator grants wallet access afterward.
</Info>

## Current points display

The count currently shown in the app's navigation and referral experience is your number of successful referrals. It is not a separate points balance.

At this stage, the app does not present a spendable points currency, redemption catalog, level system, or promised reward conversion. Treat the displayed number only as a referral count.

Successful referrals are described in [Referrals](/private-beta/referrals).
