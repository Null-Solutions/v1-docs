## Before you begin

You need a supported sign-in account and enough of a market's supported collateral in its associated wallet to enter a position. Private-beta approval does not deposit assets or open positions for you.

<Warning>
Private-beta access does not remove protocol or market risk. Review [Risks and parameters](/learn/risks-and-parameters) before depositing.
</Warning>
