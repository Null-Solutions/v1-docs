Null's private beta is for Ethereum mainnet. Access is approved for the wallet connected to your Null account, so use the same sign-in account and wallet throughout application and trading.

## Apply for access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the beta page">
    Go to `/beta` and select **Sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your details">
    Provide your name and email. If you apply as an entity, also provide its name and your title. A referral code and social handles are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the agreement">
    Confirm that the wallet shown in the app is the wallet you intend to use. Then complete the embedded DocuSeal Beta Participation Agreement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Signing submits your application. An administrator must approve your wallet on-chain before protected beta features become available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Null">
    Sign in with the same account. The app opens the beta after the connected wallet is approved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
The signed screen means your application was received. It does not mean access has already been approved.
</Info>

## Confirm your account

Before funding or trading:

1. Sign in with the same Null account used for your application.
2. Confirm the connected wallet matches the wallet named in the application.
3. Wait until that wallet has been approved before trying to use protected beta features.
4. Keep access to both your sign-in method and wallet.

After signing, the completed application screen can show your wallet and application details. A download becomes available when the signed agreement copy is ready.

If you connect a different wallet, approval for the original wallet does not automatically transfer to it.

<Warning>
Never share a seed phrase, private key, passkey, or recovery code. Null support does not need these credentials.
</Warning>

## Before you trade

Ethereum mainnet transactions require gas paid in ETH. Keep enough ETH in your approved wallet for each transaction. Market collateral is separate from gas; verify the supported collateral in the app and its contract address on the canonical [addresses page](/resources/addresses).

When your access is active, [fund your account](/private-beta/getting-started/fund-your-account). Before using real assets, review [Before you trade](/private-beta/getting-started/before-you-trade).
