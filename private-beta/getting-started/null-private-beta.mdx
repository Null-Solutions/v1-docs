Null's private beta is a limited technical test on Ethereum mainnet. Access is temporary and does not guarantee continued access or future availability. Access is tied to the wallet connected to your Null account, so use the same account and wallet when applying and trading.

## Apply for access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the beta page">
    Go to `/beta` and select **Sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your details">
    Provide your name and email. If you're applying as an entity, also provide the entity name and your title. Referral codes and social handles are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the agreement">
    Confirm that the wallet shown in the app is the wallet you intend to use. Then complete the embedded DocuSeal Beta Participation Agreement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Signing the agreement submits your application. Your wallet must then be approved onchain before beta features become available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse Null">
    While you wait, you can explore markets, portfolio views, and stats. Trading, deposits, withdrawals, and market creation stay locked until your wallet is approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return after approval">
    Sign in with the same account. Once your connected wallet is approved onchain, beta actions will unlock across the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Seeing the signed screen means your application was received, not that your access has been approved.
</Info>

## Confirm your account

Before funding or trading:

1. Sign in with the same Null account used for your application.
2. Confirm the connected wallet matches the wallet named in the application.
3. Wait until that wallet has been approved before using beta features.
4. Keep access to both your sign-in method and wallet.

After signing, you can view your wallet and application details on the completed application screen. You'll also be able to download a copy of the agreement once it's ready.

If you connect a different wallet, your approval will not automatically transfer.

<Warning>
Never share a seed phrase, private key, passkey, or recovery code. Null support does not need these credentials.
</Warning>

## Review the risks

Transactions on Ethereum mainnet require ETH for gas. Make sure your approved wallet has enough ETH before you transact. Market collateral is separate from gas; verify the supported collateral in the app and its contract address on the canonical [addresses page](/resources/addresses).

When your access is active, [fund your account](/private-beta/getting-started/fund-your-account). Before using real assets, review [Risks](/private-beta/getting-started/before-you-trade) and the [Private beta notices](/private-beta/legal/private-beta-notices).
