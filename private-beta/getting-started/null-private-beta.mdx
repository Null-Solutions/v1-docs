Null runs on Ethereum. You use your own wallet. Access and eligibility can still be restricted by jurisdiction and product rules; check the app and [Legal notices](/private-beta/legal/private-beta-notices) before you deposit.

## First steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to the Null app and select **Sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect or create a wallet">
    Connect your own wallet, or sign in with a supported login so a wallet can be set up for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete any required agreement">
    If the app asks you to sign a participation or terms agreement, complete it with the wallet you intend to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connected wallet">
    Funding, Spot, Savings, Lending, and Margin all use the wallet connected to your Null account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Home">
    Overview, Savings, Spot, Lending, and Margin are sections of the same account. See [Using the app](/private-beta/trading/using-the-app).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Completing an agreement or seeing a confirmation screen does not guarantee that every product action is unlocked in your region.
</Info>

## Confirm your account

Before funding or trading:

1. Sign in with the same Null account you intend to keep using.
2. Confirm the connected wallet is the one you funded.
3. Keep access to both your sign-in method and wallet.

If you connect a different wallet, balances and approvals do not move automatically.

<Warning>
Never share a seed phrase, private key, passkey, or recovery code. Null support does not need these credentials.
</Warning>

## Review the risks

Transactions on Ethereum require ETH for gas. Market collateral and Savings deposits are separate from gas. Verify supported tokens in the app and on the [addresses page](/resources/addresses).
