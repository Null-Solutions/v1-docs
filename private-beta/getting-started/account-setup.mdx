Use the same Null sign-in account throughout application and trading.

## Check your account

1. Sign in to Null.
2. Confirm the connected wallet is the wallet named in your beta application.
3. Wait until that wallet has been approved before trying to trade.
4. Keep access to your sign-in method and wallet.

If you signed the agreement, the completed application screen can show your wallet and application details. A download is available when the signed agreement copy is ready.

<Warning>
Never share a seed phrase, private key, passkey, or recovery code. Null support does not need these credentials.
</Warning>

When access is active, [fund your account](/private-beta/getting-started/fund-your-account).
