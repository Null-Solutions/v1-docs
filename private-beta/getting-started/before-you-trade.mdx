Null uses assets on Ethereum mainnet. Receiver and Payer Spot positions can lose most of their value. Margin Morpho positions can also be liquidated at LLTV. Review the market, contracts, and transaction preview before you deposit.

## Position risk

- **Receiver** follows the tracked oracle return, so its position value can fall when the tracked price falls.
- **Payer** is a negative-size position in the Receiver pool (market collateral inside). It takes the opposite price transfer. Lower Payer coverage means a larger short relative to Payer capital (`R / P`) and larger percentage changes in Payer share value.
- Funding is paid from collateral APR and varies with vault yield. The rate you see now is not guaranteed for the life of the position.
- **Margin** adds Morpho borrow risk and LLTV liquidation risk on top of Null pool risk.

Read [The primitive](/private-beta/about-null/the-primitive) and [Position types](/private-beta/trading/position-types) before choosing a side.

## Market and execution risk

- A transaction settles the market before collateral and shares are converted, so the final output can differ from the preview.
- Thin pools, other users' activity, fees, and slippage protections can change the outcome or prevent execution.
- Pool shares may be transferable, but that does not guarantee a secondary market or immediate liquidity.
- Null has no order book, stop orders, or configurable account leverage.

Review [Markets](/private-beta/trading/markets), [Placing a position](/private-beta/trading/placing-a-position), and [Fees](/private-beta/trading/fees).

## Oracle and circuit-breaker risk

The market settles using its configured oracle price, not the live executable price on an external venue. Missing or stale data can block settlement-dependent actions.

If an accepted price move exceeds the time-adjusted circuit-breaker threshold, the market skips the price PnL for that update and uses the new price as its next baseline. Funding can still settle. This limits the size of a specific transfer, but it does not guarantee the oracle price is correct or recover any skipped PnL.

See [Circuit breaker](/security/circuit-breaker) for the exact behavior.

## Collateral risk

Collateral can lose value independently of the tracked asset. Yield-bearing collateral also introduces additional risks around the vault, withdrawals, and conversions. Verify the collateral contract in the app against [Ethereum deployments](/resources/addresses) before transferring assets.

## Contract and administration risk

Smart-contract bugs can cause losses. Existing markets have fixed collateral, oracle, funding-rate model, and pool addresses, but immutability does not guarantee that the code is correct.

The factory owner can enable funding-rate models for future markets, set fees within contract caps, and collect reserved protocol fees. The owner cannot replace an existing market's dependencies or withdraw collateral allocated to the pools.

Nethermind Security reviewed the Null V1 contracts. An audit reduces risk, but it does not guarantee the protocol is bug-free. Read the [audit status](/private-beta/support/audits) and the full [risks and parameters](/learn/risks-and-parameters).

<Warning>
Only transact with assets you can afford to lose. Before confirming, verify the network, market, collateral, oracle, fees, pool balances, and connected wallet.
</Warning>
