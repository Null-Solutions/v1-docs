Null's private beta uses Ethereum mainnet assets. Receiver and Payer positions can lose most of their value. Review the market, contracts, and transaction preview before you Mint.

## Position risk

- **Receiver** takes the tracked oracle return and can lose when that return falls.
- **Payer** takes the opposite transfer at instantaneous leverage `L = R / P`. Lower Payer coverage means higher leverage and larger percentage moves in Payer share value.
- Funding can change sign. A rate shown now is not guaranteed for the life of a position.

Read [The primitive](/private-beta/about-null/the-primitive) and [Position types](/private-beta/trading/position-types) before choosing a side.

## Market and execution risk

- A transaction settles the market before converting collateral and shares, so confirmed output can differ from the preview.
- Thin pools, other users' flows, fees, and slippage protections can change or block execution.
- Pool shares may be transferable, but that does not guarantee a secondary market or immediate liquidity.
- Null has no order book, stop orders, or configurable account leverage.

Review [Markets](/private-beta/trading/markets), [Placing a position](/private-beta/trading/placing-a-position), and [Fees](/private-beta/trading/fees).

## Oracle and circuit-breaker risk

The market settles against its configured oracle, not an external venue's executable price. Missing or stale data can block settlement-dependent actions.

When an accepted price move exceeds the time-adjusted circuit-breaker threshold, the market skips that update's price PnL and adopts the new price as its baseline. Funding can still settle. This limits a specific transfer; it does not prove the oracle price is correct or compensate skipped PnL.

See [Circuit breaker](/security/circuit-breaker) for the exact behavior.

## Collateral risk

Collateral can lose value independently of the tracked asset. Yield-bearing collateral also introduces vault, withdrawal, and conversion risks. Verify the collateral contract in the app against [Ethereum deployments](/resources/addresses) before transferring assets.

## Contract and administration risk

Smart-contract bugs can cause loss. Existing markets fix their collateral, oracle, funding-rate model, and pool addresses, but immutability does not guarantee correct code.

The factory owner can enable funding-rate models for future markets, set fees within contract caps, and collect reserved protocol fees. The owner cannot replace an existing market's dependencies or withdraw pool-allocated collateral as an admin balance.

Null V1 has not completed a published third-party audit. Read the current [audit status](/private-beta/support/audits) and the full [risks and parameters](/learn/risks-and-parameters).

<Warning>
Only transact with assets you can afford to lose. Verify the network, market, collateral, oracle, fees, pool balances, and connected wallet before confirming.
</Warning>
