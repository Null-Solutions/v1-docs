Fund your approved wallet with the collateral supported by your selected market. You'll also need ETH in the same wallet to pay for gas on Ethereum mainnet.

Adding assets to your wallet does not open a position.

## Verify the collateral

Supported collateral can differ by market, so check your selected market's collateral in the app before funding your wallet.

Always verify the collateral's contract address (CA) against the canonical [addresses page](/resources/addresses). Don't rely solely on a token's name or symbol.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the approved wallet">
    Sign in with the account used for your beta application. Confirm that the connected wallet is the approved wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the selected market">
    In the app, check which collateral is supported by the market you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the address">
    Match the collateral contract to the canonical address before obtaining or transferring the asset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund the wallet">
    Transfer enough supported collateral for your position, plus enough ETH for gas, to your approved wallet on Ethereum mainnet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the balances">
    Wait for the transfers to confirm, then check the collateral and ETH balances before minting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prepare for gas

Ethereum mainnet transactions require ETH for gas. Keep enough ETH in your approved wallet for approvals, mints, burns, and other transactions. Your collateral and ETH balances are separate.

## Before minting

Confirm that:

- your wallet is the wallet approved for beta access;
- your wallet is connected to Ethereum mainnet;
- the collateral matches the market and the canonical address;
- the wallet holds enough collateral for the position; and
- the wallet holds enough ETH for gas.

<Warning>
Ethereum mainnet assets have real value. Always verify the network, contract address, amount, and connected wallet before confirming a transfer or transaction.
</Warning>
