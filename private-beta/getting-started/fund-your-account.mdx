Null's private beta runs on Sepolia. You fund positions with **MockUSDC** in the approved wallet connected to your Null account.

Sepolia transactions also require gas. An external wallet pays gas with Sepolia ETH. A smart account can use gas sponsorship when it is available. MockUSDC, nUSDC, and Sepolia ETH on the testnet have no cash value. Adding assets to your wallet does not open a position.

## Understand MockUSDC and nUSDC

The current Sepolia markets use `nUSDC` as collateral. `nUSDC` is an ERC-4626 vault share whose underlying token is `MockUSDC`.

You fund the wallet with `MockUSDC`. When the router deposits into a market that uses ERC-4626 collateral, it pulls the underlying `MockUSDC`, wraps it into `nUSDC`, and deposits the `nUSDC` into the market. On redemption through the router, it unwraps `nUSDC` and returns `MockUSDC`.

<Info>
`MockUSDC` is the test token you hold and spend through the app flow. `nUSDC` is the yield-bearing collateral token held by the market. They are different ERC-20 tokens with different Sepolia addresses.
</Info>

You can verify both token addresses in [Live deployments](/resources/addresses).

## Get test collateral

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the approved wallet">
    Sign in with the account used for your beta application. Confirm that the connected wallet is the approved wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the deposit menu">
    Open your portfolio and select **Deposit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund with testnet assets">
    Select **Testnet Fund** and confirm the transaction to mint MockUSDC to your connected wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your balance">
    Wait for the Sepolia transaction to confirm before trying to mint a position.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prepare for gas

If you use an external wallet, keep Sepolia ETH in that wallet to pay gas for faucet, approval, mint, and burn transactions. Minting MockUSDC does not provide Sepolia ETH.

If you use a smart account, the app can submit sponsored transactions when Sepolia gas sponsorship is configured. If sponsorship is unavailable, the transaction cannot rely on MockUSDC or nUSDC to pay gas.

## Before minting

Confirm all four:

- your wallet is the wallet approved for beta access;
- your wallet is connected to Sepolia;
- it holds enough MockUSDC for the position; and
- your external wallet has Sepolia ETH for gas, or gas sponsorship is available for your smart account.

<Warning>
Do not send mainnet USDC, mainnet ETH, or other assets to a Sepolia token contract. Always verify the network, token, and connected wallet before confirming a transaction.
</Warning>
