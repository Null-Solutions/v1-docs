Fund the approved wallet connected to your Null account with the selected market's supported collateral. You also need ETH in that wallet to pay Ethereum mainnet gas.

Adding assets to your wallet does not open a position.

## Verify the collateral

Supported collateral can differ by market. In the app, check the selected market's collateral before funding your wallet.

Verify its contract address against the canonical [addresses page](/resources/addresses). Token names and symbols alone are not sufficient to identify a contract.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the approved wallet">
    Sign in with the account used for your beta application. Confirm that the connected wallet is the approved wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the selected market">
    In the app, identify the collateral supported by the market you intend to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the address">
    Match the collateral contract with the canonical address before obtaining or transferring the asset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund the wallet">
    Transfer enough supported collateral for your position and enough ETH for gas to the approved wallet on Ethereum mainnet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the balances">
    Wait for the transfers to confirm, then check the collateral and ETH balances before minting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prepare for gas

Ethereum mainnet transactions use ETH for gas. Keep enough ETH in the approved wallet for approvals, mints, burns, and other required transactions. Your collateral balance is separate from your ETH balance.

## Before minting

Confirm that:

- your wallet is the wallet approved for beta access;
- your wallet is connected to Ethereum mainnet;
- the collateral matches the market and the canonical address;
- the wallet holds enough collateral for the position; and
- the wallet holds enough ETH for gas.

<Warning>
Ethereum mainnet assets have real value. Always verify the network, contract address, amount, and connected wallet before confirming a transfer or transaction.
</Warning>
