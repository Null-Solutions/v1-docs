Fund your connected wallet with the assets you need. Adding assets to your wallet does not open a position.

## Cash, Savings, and market collateral

| Need | Asset |
| --- | --- |
| **Cash** in the app | USDC in your wallet |
| **Savings** | USDC to deposit into the Null Savings Vault |
| **Spot / Payer** | The market's collateral (often USDC wrapped to a Morpho vault share by the router) |
| **Gas** | ETH on Ethereum |

Always verify contract addresses against the [addresses page](/resources/addresses). Do not rely only on a token name or symbol.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the wallet">
    Sign in and confirm the connected wallet is the one you will fund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the product you want">
    Savings uses USDC. Spot markets show their collateral in the app. Margin and Lending may need USDC and/or pool shares.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the address">
    Match each token to the canonical address before transferring.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund the wallet">
    Transfer enough tokens for the action, plus enough ETH for gas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm balances">
    Wait for confirmations, then check balances in the wallet and in Home.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prepare for gas

Keep enough ETH for approvals, deposits, redeems, Morpho opens/closes, and other transactions. Collateral and ETH balances are separate.

<Warning>
Onchain assets have real value. Verify the network, contract address, amount, and connected wallet before confirming a transfer.
</Warning>
