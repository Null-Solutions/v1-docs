Funding your account adds assets to your connected wallet. It does not open a market position.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Deposit">
    Sign in, open your portfolio, and select **Deposit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a method">
    Select **Use Crypto** to use Privy's crypto deposit flow, or **Use Cash** to open the fiat onramp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the provider steps">
    Confirm the destination wallet, asset, network, and amount before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the balance">
    Cash and cross-chain deposits can take time to arrive. Check your wallet balance before placing a position.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Testnet faucet

When Null is running on a testnet, **Testnet Fund** appears in the deposit menu. Confirm the transaction to mint testnet USDC to your wallet. Testnet assets have no cash value.

## Withdraw funds

The portfolio withdrawal menu supports **Receive Crypto** to an external address and **Receive Cash** through the available off-ramp. Verify every destination and network before confirming.

<Warning>
Funds sent with the wrong token, network, or destination address may be permanently lost.
</Warning>
