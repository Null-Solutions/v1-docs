Open `/nullpoints` after signing in to review your NullPoints balance, earning breakdown, progress toward your next referral link, and referral activity.

<Warning>
NullPoints and referral-link progression are currently a preview. Values shown in the app may be placeholders until the live rewards system launches.
</Warning>

## Earn NullPoints from positions

You earn **1 NullPoint per $1 of eligible open-position portfolio value per hour**.

For example, an eligible position value of $2,500 held for one hour generates 2,500 NullPoints. As your open-position value changes, the rate at which you earn points changes with it.

The app separates:

- **From your positions** — points generated by your own eligible open positions.
- **From referrals** — bonus points generated by wallets that joined through your referral links.

## Unlock referral links

Every 10,000 NullPoints unlocks another referral link. The progress indicator shows how many points remain before your next link becomes available.

Available links appear in the referral-code list. Copy a link and send it to the wallet you want to invite. A link becomes active after the invited wallet uses it and completes the required private-beta access steps.

## Earn 20% from referrals

After a referral becomes active, you earn referral points equal to **20% of the NullPoints that the referred wallet earns**.

For example, if a referred wallet earns 1,000 NullPoints from its positions, you earn 200 referral NullPoints.

The referral list shows:

- active referral codes first;
- the wallet associated with each active code;
- the NullPoints each active referral has generated for you; and
- unused links that are ready to share.

Your own wallet cannot use its referral link. An invite must be associated with a distinct wallet.

<Note>
NullPoints have no guaranteed cash value, token conversion, or payout. Null may change eligibility rules, earning rates, referral requirements, or program availability before launch.
</Note>
