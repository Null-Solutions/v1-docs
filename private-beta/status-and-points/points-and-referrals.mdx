NullPoints are a private-beta participation metric. They are intended to help bring enough test capital and activity into Receiver and Payer pools to evaluate the UI and protocol under realistic conditions.

They are not part of a position's financial return. NullPoints do not increase your pool-share value, change protocol settlement, or represent interest, yield, a fee rebate, or compensation from Null.

Open `/nullpoints` after signing in to review the preview balance, calculation breakdown, progress toward an additional invitation link, and referral activity.

<Warning>
NullPoints and invitation-link progression are currently a preview. Values shown in the app may be placeholders. Participation remains invitation-only and subject to the Private Beta Agreement and eligibility requirements.
</Warning>

## Preview earning model

The preview records **1 NullPoint per $1 of eligible open-position value per hour**.

For example, an eligible position value of $2,500 held for one hour records 2,500 NullPoints. This measurement is designed to reflect the amount and duration of capital participating in the protocol test. As open-position value changes, the recorded rate changes with it.

The app separates:

- **From your positions** — points attributed to your own eligible open positions.
- **From referrals** — additional points attributed to eligible activity by participants who joined through your invitation links.

## Eligibility during the preview

Final eligibility rules are not live yet. The current preview does not establish whether a particular Receiver or Payer position, market status, collateral type, or time period will qualify when the rewards system launches.

Until the live rules are published:

- treat displayed position and referral points as illustrative;
- do not assume wallet collateral that has not been Minted earns points;
- do not assume closed, burned, transferred, or otherwise inactive positions continue earning; and
- do not make financial decisions based on a projected points balance.

This page will be updated with the eligibility snapshot method, accrual cadence, excluded positions, and launch date before live rewards begin.

## Additional invitation links

The preview makes another invitation link available for every 10,000 NullPoints recorded. The progress indicator shows the preview balance remaining before the next link.

These links help assemble a sufficiently varied group of participants and pool conditions for technical testing. A link does not guarantee access. It becomes active only after a distinct invited participant uses it and completes the required private-beta access and approval steps.

## Referral attribution

After a referral becomes active, the preview attributes additional points equal to **20% of the NullPoints recorded for the referred participant's eligible positions**.

For example, if 1,000 NullPoints are recorded for a referred participant's eligible positions, the preview attributes 200 referral NullPoints to you.

The referral list shows:

- active referral codes first;
- the wallet associated with each active code;
- the NullPoints each active referral has generated for you; and
- unused links that are ready to share.

Your own wallet cannot use its referral link. An invite must be associated with a distinct wallet.

<Note>
NullPoints are non-transferable accounting entries for the private-beta test. They are not money, a cryptoasset, a claim against Null, or a promise of any token, cash value, conversion, allocation, or payout. Null may change or discontinue the calculation, eligibility rules, invitation thresholds, referral attribution, or program before launch.
</Note>
