## Season 0

**Season 0 has started.** The app's `/points` page monitors normal trading activity. That activity counts toward points shown on the page.

Points are **non-transferable accounting entries**. They are not money, not yield, and not a promise of any token, allocation, airdrop, or payout.

## Where to look

| Surface | What you get today |
| --- | --- |
| `/points` | Season 0 hero and monitoring notice |
| Docs link from the page | External "Read about Points" resource when published |
| `/referrals` | Redirects to `/points` |

A fuller rewards and referrals UI may ship later. Until then, treat Season 0 as activity monitoring toward points, not as a live redeemable balance product.

## Referrals

Referral mechanics, if enabled in your account, are described in the app and any linked Points article. Do not assume a fixed bonus from docs alone.

<Warning>
Points have no cash value in these docs. Do not treat them as compensation, interest, or an investment return.
</Warning>
