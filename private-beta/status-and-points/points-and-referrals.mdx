## Your Points

Open `/points` after signing in to view your Points total, earning breakdown, progress toward your next referral code, and referral activity.

## How you earn

Null records **1 Point / Portfolio Value / Hour** from open positions.

The Points page shows:

- **From positions:** points earned from your open positions.
- **From referrals:** points earned from referrals.
- **Until next code:** points remaining before your next referral code unlocks.

## Referral codes

You unlock a new referral link every **10,000 points**.

Active referrals appear first, followed by available invite links. The referral list shows:

- **Code:** the referral code.
- **Status:** **Active** or **Available**.
- **Referral:** the referred wallet for an active code.
- **Points earned:** the referral points attributed to you.

Use the copy action to share an available invite link. Sign in to generate referral codes.

<Note>
  Points are non-transferable participation metrics for the private beta only.
  They have no monetary value and cannot be bought, sold, exchanged, redeemed,
  or converted for cash, cryptoassets, tokens, or anything else of value. They
  do not create any right to a reward, allocation, payment, or benefit, and they
  are not a roadmap commitment. Null may change or end Points at any time.
</Note>
