Null does not currently have a true points economy.

The number shown in the referral experience counts successful referrals. The app does not currently provide:

- a separate points balance;
- points earned from trading or deposits;
- levels or tiers;
- a redemption catalog; or
- a promised conversion, payout, or reward value.

To increase the displayed referral count, follow [Referrals](/private-beta/status-and-points/referrals).

<Info>
Treat the number only as a successful-referral count unless the official app and documentation are updated.
</Info>
