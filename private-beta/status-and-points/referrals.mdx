Open `/referrals` after signing in to view your referral code, copy your invite link, and see your successful-referral total.

## Refer a wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your invite link">
    Use the copy control beside your referral code. The link opens `/beta` with your code filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the link">
    The invitee completes their application and DocuSeal agreement with the referral code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    The invited wallet must receive private-beta access before it counts as successful.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When a referral counts

A referral is successful only after a distinct wallet:

1. opens the private-beta application with your referral code;
2. completes the application;
3. signs the DocuSeal agreement; and
4. receives private-beta approval for that wallet.

Your own wallet cannot refer itself. Sending a link, starting an application, or signing the agreement does not increase the total before approval.

If an expected referral is missing, ask the invitee to confirm that they used your code and completed the application with the same wallet that was approved.

<Note>
The displayed number is only a successful-referral count. Null does not currently provide a separate points balance or guarantee a reward, payout, or conversion value for referrals.
</Note>
