Open `/referrals` after signing in to view your referral code, copy your invite link, and see your successful-referral total.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your invite link">
    Use the copy control beside your referral code. The link opens `/beta` with your code filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the link">
    The invitee completes their application and DocuSeal agreement with the referral code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    The invited wallet must receive private-beta access before it counts as successful.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A distinct wallet counts after it applies with your code, signs the agreement, and is approved. Your own wallet cannot refer itself.

<Note>
Successful referrals increase the displayed count. They do not currently create a separate points balance or guaranteed reward.
</Note>
