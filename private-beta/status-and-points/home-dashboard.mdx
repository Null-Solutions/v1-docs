The signed-in home experience brings together your wallet and market activity.

Use it to:

- check portfolio value and balances;
- review minted positions and history;
- return to saved markets;
- deposit or withdraw wallet funds; and
- open markets to mint or burn positions.

The referral number shown in the app is your successful-referral count. It is not a spendable points balance.

If you have applied but cannot open protected beta features, your wallet may still be waiting for on-chain approval. Review [Null Private Beta](/private-beta/getting-started/null-private-beta).
