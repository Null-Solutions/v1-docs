A Null V1 market is uniquely identified by three immutable dependency addresses:

```text
(collateral, oracle, frm)
```

The factory permits only one market for each tuple. Changing any dependency means creating a different market.

## Immutable identity

<ParamField path="collateral" type="IERC20" required>
  The ERC-20 held by the market and used by both ERC-4626 pools. Pool assets,
  fees, funding, and price PnL are denominated in raw units of this token. See
  [Collateral](/markets/collateral).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="oracle" type="INullV1Oracle" required>
  The contract that supplies the tracked asset price, normalized to `1e36`.
  Settlement requires a nonzero price. See [Oracles](/markets/oracles).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="frm" type="INullV1FundingRateModel" required>
  The funding-rate model queried during every settlement. The factory owner must
  enable the FRM before a market can use it. An enabled FRM cannot be disabled.
</ParamField>

<Warning>
  A market does not have an `asset` or `mu` identity field. Its identity is
  `(collateral, oracle, frm)`. Funding parameters such as `mu` belong to the FRM
  implementation.
</Warning>

## Other fixed addresses

<ParamField path="factory" type="address">
  The `NullV1Factory` that cloned and registered the market. The factory owner
  controls fees and FRM enablement but cannot replace the market dependencies.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="receiverPool" type="INullV1Pool">
  The ERC-4626 pool whose assets receive the tracked price return.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="payerPool" type="INullV1Pool">
  The ERC-4626 reserve pool that backs Receiver gains and takes the opposite
  price exposure.
</ParamField>

The factory clones both pools during market initialization and emits their addresses in `CreateMarket`.

## Mutable fee parameters

The factory owner can update three market fees. Each is expressed in basis points and capped at 500 bp:

| Accessor          | Charged on                                | Maximum |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------: |
| `entryFee()`      | `deposit` and `mint`                      |      5% |
| `exitFee()`       | `withdraw` and `redeem`                   |      5% |
| `collateralFee()` | Pool assets, annualized during settlement |  5% APR |

All fees accrue in `protocolFees`. `sweepFees()` settles the market first, then transfers accrued fees to the factory owner.

## Standard imbalance FRM

`NullV1ImbalanceFundingRateModel` has two immutable, WAD-scaled constructor parameters:

<ParamField path="mu" type="uint256" required>
  Funding sensitivity and the absolute rate bound. It must satisfy
  `0 < mu <= 1e18`. The default deployment uses `1e18`, meaning `mu = 1`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="targetCoverage" type="uint256" required>
  Target Payer coverage `c = P/R`. It must satisfy
  `0 < targetCoverage <= 1e18`. The default is `0.5e18`, meaning `c = 0.5`.
</ParamField>

For prepared Receiver assets `R` and Payer assets `P`, the signed WAD APR is:

```text
f = mu * (cR - P) / (cR + P)
```

- Positive `f` means Receiver pays Payer.
- Negative `f` means Payer pays Receiver.
- Funding is zero at `P = cR`.
- Funding is zero if either pool is empty.
- `|f| <= mu` by construction; there is no separate runtime cap.
- Payer capital APR is `f * R / P`.

The standard FRM is stateless. A market can instead reference another enabled implementation of `INullV1FundingRateModel`, so integrations should identify the FRM before interpreting a rate.

## Default-model examples

With `R = 1,000`, `c = 0.5`, and `mu = 1`:

| Payer assets `P` | Coverage `P/R` |    `f` |      Annual transfer | Payer capital APR |
| ---------------: | -------------: | -----: | -------------------: | ----------------: |
|               50 |             5% | +81.8% | 818 Receiver → Payer |           +1,636% |
|              100 |            10% | +66.7% | 667 Receiver → Payer |             +667% |
|              200 |            20% | +42.9% | 429 Receiver → Payer |             +214% |
|              500 |            50% |     0% |                    0 |                0% |
|              750 |            75% | -20.0% | 200 Payer → Receiver |            -26.7% |
|            1,000 |           100% | -33.3% | 333 Payer → Receiver |            -33.3% |

At `P = 100`, the annual transfer is 667—not 429:

```text
f = (500 - 100) / (500 + 100) = 66.7%
66.7% * 1,000 = 667
```

Actual transfers are prorated by elapsed time, netted with price PnL, and capped by the paying pool's settleable assets.

## Reading parameters onchain

```solidity
INullV1Market market = INullV1Market(marketAddress);

address factory      = market.factory();
address collateral   = address(market.collateral());
address oracle       = address(market.oracle());
address frm          = address(market.frm());
address receiverPool = address(market.receiverPool());
address payerPool    = address(market.payerPool());

uint16 entryFee      = market.entryFee();
uint16 exitFee       = market.exitFee();
uint16 collateralFee = market.collateralFee();
```

For the standard FRM, query its parameters separately:

```solidity
NullV1ImbalanceFundingRateModel model =
    NullV1ImbalanceFundingRateModel(frm);

uint256 mu = model.mu();
uint256 targetCoverage = model.targetCoverage();
```

Use `factory.getMarket(collateral, oracle, frm)` to discover the canonical market. See [Factory reference](/contracts/factory) and [Market update](/developers-tab/market-update) for the registry and settlement flow.
