A market is uniquely identified by three dependency addresses fixed during initialization:

```text
(collateral, oracle, frm)
```

The factory creates at most one market per tuple. A live market has no setter for any dependency. To use different collateral, oracle, or FRM, create a new tuple and therefore a new market.

Product funding is **yield share of collateral APR** (Receivers pay Payers; never the reverse in the app model). The `frm` address remains part of market identity and is still queried at settlement.

## Immutable identity

<ParamField path="collateral" type="IERC20" required>
  ERC-20 held by the market and both pools. Accounting is in raw units of this token. It is expected to have 18 decimals, because the pool floor `MIN_POOL_ASSETS` is a fixed `1e18` raw units. See [Collateral](/markets/collateral).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="oracle" type="INullV1Oracle" required>
  Tracked asset price at `1e36`. Settlement requires nonzero. See [Oracles](/markets/oracles).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="frm" type="INullV1FundingRateModel" required>
  Funding-rate model queried every settlement. The factory must enable it before market creation. Product economics treat funding as a share of collateral APR paid to Payers; see [Funding and carry](/how-null-works/payers-earn-interest-and-fees).
</ParamField>

<Warning>
A market has no `asset()` identity accessor. Read `market.collateral()` for its collateral token. Each Receiver and Payer pool implements ERC-4626, so `pool.asset()` returns that same market collateral. Read `market.frm()` before interpreting onchain rate calls.
</Warning>

The factory has no FRM disable function. Even if factory governance enables another FRM later, the `frm()` of an existing market does not change.

## Other fixed addresses

| Field | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `factory` | Cloned/registered the market; owner sets fees and enables FRMs |
| `receiverPool` | ERC-4626 pool that receives the tracked price return |
| `payerPool` | ERC-4626 reserve with the opposite price exposure |

Both pools are cloned at init and emitted in `CreateMarket`.

## Mutable fees

Factory owner can set two fees with `setFees(newEntryFee, newExitFee)`. Entry and exit default to 25 bps and are capped at 250 bps.

| Accessor | Charged on | Maximum |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| `entryFee()` | `deposit`, on settleable-R notional | 250 bps (2.5%) |
| `exitFee()` | `redeem`, same notional | 250 bps (2.5%) |

Receiver-side fees credit the Payer pool (or the Receiver pool while the Payer pool is at or below `MIN_POOL_ASSETS`). Payer-side fees credit `protocolFees`. `sweepFees()` pays the factory owner the `protocolFees` recorded at the last settlement; it does not settle the market. Details: [Fees](/protocol/fees).

## Funding model (product)

For UI and carry math, treat funding as:

```text
f = s * collateral APR    (s = yield share; app uses s = 1)
annual funding transfer = f * R    (Receiver → Payer)
```

- Funding is never negative in the product model (Payers do not pay Receivers).
- Coverage still sets the Payer short scale `R / P` (negative Receiver size vs Payer capital) and scales how funding lands per unit of Payer.
- Lens returns both `fundingRate` and `collateralRate` for display.

The funding model in use is [NullV1YieldShareFundingRateModel](/protocol/funding-rate-model). Always read `market.frm()` onchain. A different enabled model would be a different market tuple.

## Read onchain

```solidity
address factory      = market.factory();
address collateral   = address(market.collateral());
address oracle       = address(market.oracle());
address frm          = address(market.frm());
address receiverPool = address(market.receiverPool());
address payerPool    = address(market.payerPool());

uint16 entryFee      = market.entryFee();
uint16 exitFee       = market.exitFee();
```

Discover with `factory.getMarket(collateral, oracle, frm)`. Settlement order: [Settlement](/protocol/settlement). Morpho periphery: [Morpho](/how-null-works/synthetics-go-on-morpho).
