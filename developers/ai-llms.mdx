## Terms to preserve

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Receiver** | Pool that receives the oracle return and pays collateral yield as funding |
| **Payer** | Negative-size position in the Receiver pool, with the market's collateral inside it. Short scale is `R/P` |
| **Coverage `P/R`** | Payer assets divided by Receiver assets |
| **FRM** | Funding-rate model stored on the market. Current model pays vault yield to Payers and does not go negative |
| **Funding** | Vault yield on the Receiver pool, paid to Payers |
| **Buy / Sell** | App words for pool deposit and redeem |
| **nUSDC** | Share of the Null Collateral Vault, a Morpho Vault V2 over USDC. Collateral in every market |
| **Null Savings Vault** | Morpho vault that holds `nUSDC` and lends it into each market's long book |
| **Long / short book** | Morpho markets per synthetic: `rX \| nUSDC` lends `nUSDC` against Receiver shares; `nUSDC \| rX` lends Receiver shares against `nUSDC` |
| **Margin** | Morpho long or short via `NullV1MorphoRouter` |

## Guardrails

- Discover markets by `(collateral, oracle, frm)`. Do not invent pool addresses.
- Read pool addresses from the market or from `CreateMarket`.
- Keep coverage `P/R` distinct from short scale `R/P`. Neither is a funding rate. Do not describe Payer as a leverage slider.
- Handle zero denominators. Do not report either ratio as finite when its denominator is zero.
- Treat shares as ERC-4626. Share decimals equal collateral decimals plus 10.
- Assume every pool write settles the market first.
- Pass slippage floors and deadlines on router calls.
- Route Chainlink updates through `NullV1Router`. Do not attach `msg.value`.
- Convert units when collateral is ERC-4626. The router takes underlying. Pool previews take vault shares.
- Fetch signed oracle payloads from Chainlink. Never fabricate `updateData`.
- Do not invent a Null SDK, a REST trading API, or unpublished addresses.
- Copy constants from [Deployments](/developers/addresses) only after they are listed. If a constant is missing, stop.

## What not to invent

There is no published partner API, no order book, and no Null liquidation engine. Spot gains move only from collateral already in the market. Margin liquidation is Morpho's, at that book's LLTV.

Factory `createMarket` does not take an allow-list argument. Do not generate approval checks that the current factory does not have.
