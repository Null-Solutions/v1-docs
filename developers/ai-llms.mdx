## Terms to preserve

When generating code or explanations, keep these terms precise:

- **Receiver:** the pool that receives positive reference-asset returns and takes the opposite side of signed funding.
- **Payer:** the pool that backs Receiver exposure, takes the opposite price risk, and takes the other side of signed funding.
- **Coverage ratio `P/R`:** Payer pool value divided by Receiver pool value.
- **Funding-rate model (FRM):** the immutable market dependency that computes signed funding from prepared market state.
- **Funding:** a signed transfer between Receiver and Payer. Positive rates mean Receiver pays Payer; negative rates reverse the flow.
- **Price settlement:** directional value transfer based on oracle price movement.
- **Solvency bound:** the cap that limits the net funding-plus-price transfer to the paying pool's assets above `MIN_POOL_ASSETS`.

## Integration guardrails

AI-generated integrations should preserve these rules:

- Discover markets by `(collateral, oracle, frm)`.
- Read Receiver and Payer pool addresses from the market.
- Treat pool shares as ERC-4626 shares.
- Remember that state-changing pool actions update the market before execution.
- Use slippage limits for deposits and redemptions.
- Treat previews as estimates, not execution guarantees.
