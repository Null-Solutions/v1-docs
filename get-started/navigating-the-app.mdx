Everything starts at Home. The four products carry different risks, but they belong to one wallet and one portfolio, so Home shows them side by side without blending them together.

At the top, the portfolio figure combines your open positions with your Cash. **Invested** is what's working across the products. **Cash value** is the USDC still in your wallet.

## Home

On a large screen, sections appear as pills across Home. On a phone, the logo opens **Sections**.

| Section | Address | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | `/` | The whole portfolio, its chart, and a row for each product plus Cash. |
| **Savings** | `/?section=savings` | The **Null Savings Vault**, with **Add money** and **Withdraw**. |
| **Spot** | `/?section=spot` | Your pool positions. |
| **Lending** | `/?section=lending` | What you've lent, with a link to the full book. |
| **Margin** | `/?section=margin` | The Margin desk. It lives here and nowhere else. |

A product you haven't used says so plainly: **No savings yet**, **No spot yet**, **No lending yet**, **No margin yet**. **Invest** on Home asks which product you want.

## Other routes

| Route | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| `/trade` | Spot markets. Open a row for **Buy**, **Sell** and **Receive**. |
| `/lending` | The full lending book, with **Lend** and **Recall** on each market. |
| `/points` | Season 0. See [Points](/points). |

Margin is not on `/trade`. Go to **Home** and then **Margin**.

## Pro Mode

The account menu holds **Pro Mode**. With it on, Spot also shows Payer, the side that backs Receivers and behaves very differently. If you only want to follow prices, leave it off.

Each product has its own guide: [Savings](/products/savings), [Spot](/products/spot), [Lending](/products/lending) and [Margin](/products/margin).
