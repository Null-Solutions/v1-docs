The app is built around Home, and that choice tells you how Null thinks
about your money. Savings, Spot, Lending, and Margin are separate products
with separate risks, but they belong to one wallet and one portfolio. Home
keeps them side by side without blending them into a single balance, so you
can see what you own in each place and what is still sitting idle.

At the top of Home, the portfolio figure combines the current value of your
open positions with your Cash. **Invested** is the part already working
across the four products, while **Cash value** is the USDC still in your
wallet. Watching those two numbers move against each other is the quickest
way to tell whether money is deployed or simply waiting.

## The sections on Home

On a large screen, the sections appear as pills across Home. On a phone,
the logo opens **Sections**, and the same destinations appear there.
**Overview** comes first and gives you the whole picture: the portfolio,
its chart, and one row each for Savings, Spot, Lending, Margin, and Cash.

Each remaining section narrows the view to a single product. **Savings**
shows the **Null Savings Vault** with **Add money** and **Withdraw**. **Spot**
shows your pool positions. **Lending** shows what you have supplied to
Morpho books, with a link onward to the full book. **Margin** opens the
Margin desk, which lives here and nowhere else in the app.

| Section | URL | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | `/` | The full portfolio, its chart, and a row for each product plus Cash |
| **Savings** | `/?section=savings` | The **Null Savings Vault**, with **Add money** and **Withdraw** |
| **Spot** | `/?section=spot` | Your pool positions at a glance |
| **Lending** | `/?section=lending` | Your Morpho supplies, with a link to the full book |
| **Margin** | `/?section=margin` | The Margin desk for leveraged positions |

If you have not used a product yet, its row says so plainly rather than
showing an empty chart. You will see **No savings yet**, **No spot yet**,
**No lending yet**, and **No margin yet**. Those rows are an invitation,
not an error. Choosing **Invest** on Home asks which product you want, and
offers to add money to Savings, buy shares, lend, or open a margin position.

## Routes beyond Home

Some work is better done away from the portfolio view. The `/trade` route
lists the Spot markets, and opening a row gives you **Buy**, **Sell**, and
**Receive** for that market. The `/lending` route carries the full lending
book, where **Lend** and **Recall** act on each book directly. The `/points`
route tracks Season 0, which the [Points](/points) page explains.

Margin is the exception worth remembering. It is only available through
Home and then **Margin**, and you will not find it on `/trade`. If you go
looking for leverage among the Spot markets, you are in the wrong place.

## One setting that changes what you see

The account menu holds **Pro Mode**, and it matters more than its quiet
placement suggests. With Pro Mode on, Spot also shows Payer, the pool that
backs Receiver and behaves very differently from it. If you only want
Receiver exposure, leave Pro Mode off and Payer stays out of your way.
