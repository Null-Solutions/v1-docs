Connect a wallet you control on Ethereum mainnet with **Sign in**. That wallet holds your positions and signs your transactions. Null never holds your funds, so it cannot recover a wallet or reverse a transaction. Null is not offered in the United Kingdom, and the app blocks sanctioned regions. See [Legal notices](/legal-notices).

There is no account to top up. **Cash** is the USDC already in the wallet you connected. When a product needs money, it spends from there. When you withdraw or sell, the USDC comes back to the same wallet. Cash earns nothing until you put it to work.

## How each product uses Cash

| Product | Action | Where your USDC goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Savings | **Add money** | Becomes nUSDC in the Null Savings Vault, which lends it to the long markets. |
| Spot | **Buy** | Becomes nUSDC, the Collateral Vault share, and goes into a market's pool. |
| Lending | **Lend** | Supplied as nUSDC to a long market. Or lend a synthetic you hold to its short market. |
| Margin | **Place Order** | Posted as the equity behind a **Long** or **Short**. |
| Anything | Gas | Paid in ETH on Ethereum mainnet. |

A wallet full of USDC and empty of ETH can see its balance but do nothing with it. Treat a small ETH buffer as part of funding.

## Get USDC to your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your address">
    Open **Receive** and copy your address. Read the network notice before you send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send USDC on Ethereum">
    From an exchange, a bridge or another wallet. USDC sent on another chain won't appear as Cash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep some ETH">
    Your next deposit, sale or withdrawal needs gas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a product">
    Back on Home, your USDC shows under **Cash**. **Invest** lets you pick Savings, Spot, Lending or Margin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Withdrawals and sells return USDC to your wallet, subject to the liquidity free at the time and any fees. If a withdrawal is smaller than you expected, check what was free to leave first.
