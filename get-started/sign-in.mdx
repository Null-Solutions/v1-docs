Null runs on Ethereum mainnet, and signing in means connecting a wallet you
already control. There is no Null account sitting between you and your assets,
and no internal balance waiting to be funded. The wallet you connect is the
wallet that holds your positions, signs your transactions, and receives
anything you later withdraw or sell back to USDC.

That simplicity is worth pausing on, because it shapes everything that
follows. If you lose access to your wallet, Null cannot restore it for you.
If you send assets to the wrong network, they will not appear in the app.
And if you connect a different wallet later, you will be looking at a
different portfolio, even though the app itself looks exactly the same.

## What to have ready

Before you connect, it helps to have three things in place. You will need a
wallet you control and can sign with. You will need USDC in that wallet,
because USDC is the cash that Savings, Spot, Lending, and Margin all draw
from. Finally, keep a little ETH in the same wallet for gas, since every
transaction on Ethereum mainnet costs ETH to confirm.

You should also be sure you are allowed to use Null where you are. Null is
not offered in the United Kingdom, and the app blocks sanctioned regions.
The trade ticket carries the line **By trading you agree to the Terms of
Service and Privacy Policy**, and the [Legal notices](/legal) page explains
the main limits in plain language. If those notices and an agreement shown
in the app ever differ, the agreement is the one that controls.

## Connecting your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app and start the connection">
    Choose **Sign in** and select your wallet when the app asks how you want
    to connect. Approving that prompt links the address to your session. It
    does not move any funds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm you are on Ethereum">
    The app should show Ethereum as the network. If your wallet asks you to
    sign on a different network, stop and check before approving. Null
    positions live on Ethereum mainnet, and activity elsewhere will not show
    up here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the address, not just the connection">
    The connected address is where positions will be held and where proceeds
    will return. If you keep funds across several wallets, take a second to
    confirm you connected the one you actually intend to trade from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund Cash when you are ready">
    Once connected, USDC in that wallet appears as **Cash**. Nothing else is
    required to get started, and [Fund your account](/get-started/fund-your-account)
    walks through getting USDC to the right place.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Receiving assets into the wallet

You will see **Receive** on Spot and among the home actions. It shows the
address of the wallet you connected, so you or an exchange can send assets
to it. A transfer sent there on the selected chain arrives as an ordinary
wallet transfer. It does not open a position on its own, and choosing
**Receive** does not commit you to anything.

<Warning>
Null cannot recover a wallet, reverse a confirmed transaction, or move funds
without your signature. Check the network and the address before you send.
</Warning>

With your wallet connected and Cash in place, the next step is learning your
way around. Continue to [Navigating the app](/get-started/navigating-the-app).
