Null is on Ethereum mainnet. You use your own wallet. There is no separate Null balance and no custodial account.

## Before you start

- A wallet you control.
- USDC for **Cash**, Savings, or collateral.
- A little ETH for gas.
- Confirmation that you are allowed to use the app where you are. Null is not offered in the United Kingdom, and the app blocks sanctioned regions.

The trade ticket includes **By trading you agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.** The [Legal notices](/legal) summarize the main limits. If those notices and an agreement in the app ever differ, the agreement controls.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to the Null app and choose **Sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a wallet">
    Approve the connection in your wallet. The address you connect is the address that holds positions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the network">
    The app should show Ethereum. Reject a prompt that asks you to sign on a different network unless you meant to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund Cash">
    USDC in that wallet is **Cash**. See [Fund your account](/get-started/fund-your-account).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Receive**, on Spot and on the home actions, shows this wallet's address. Assets sent there on the selected chain arrive as a normal transfer.

<Warning>
Null cannot recover a wallet, reverse a transaction, or move funds without your signature.
</Warning>
