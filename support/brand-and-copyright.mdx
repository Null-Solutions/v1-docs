Null Solutions Limited owns the Null brand assets published in these docs, the Null name and logos as used for the product, and the text and diagrams in this documentation site. This page explains what that means for third-party use. It does not replace the product [Legal notices](/legal-notices).

## Trademarks

Null and related names, logos and marks are trademarks of Null Solutions Limited. Do not use them in a way that suggests Null Solutions Limited sponsors, endorses or partners with you, your product or your service, unless you have written permission.

A software licence for protocol or application code does not grant trademark rights. Using open or source-available Null code does not let you use Null branding as if it were your own.

## Brand kit assets

The official downloadable logos live on the [Brand kit](/support/brand-kit) page. You may use those files to identify Null accurately when you write about the protocol, link to it or present it, as long as you:

- Use the official files from the brand kit, not screenshots or redrawn copies
- Keep the artwork's proportions and clear space
- Use only the four allowed pairings: green on sage, sage on green, warm black on white and white on warm black
- Make clear that Null is a product of Null Solutions Limited when context would otherwise be ambiguous

That permitted use is limited. It covers accurate reference and identification. It is not a licence to build a competing brand on Null assets, and it is not approval for co-marketing.

## What you may not do

Do not:

- Imply a partnership, endorsement, affiliation or official Null product without written permission
- Modify, recolour or distort the logos, or combine them with other marks in a way that changes their meaning
- Place the mark on terracotta, fjord blue or sand, or on any pairing outside the four allowed
- Publish unofficial or reconstructed artwork as official Null materials
- Use Null logos or names as the primary brand for your own app, site, token, bot or service
- Register domains, social handles or app store listings that trade on Null branding in a confusing way

If you are unsure whether a use is allowed, ask before you publish.

## Documentation content

The explanatory text, structure and diagrams on this site are provided so you can learn and integrate with Null. You may quote short passages with attribution when discussing the protocol. Do not republish the docs wholesale, mirror them as your own product documentation or strip Null Solutions Limited attribution from substantial copies.

## Software and smart contract licences are separate

Brand and documentation rights are separate from licences that cover Null software and smart contracts. Contract and application repositories ship with their own `LICENSE` files. Those licences govern code. They do not expand or replace the brand rules on this page. Where a code licence later becomes GPL or another open-source licence, that still does not grant trademark or logo rights.

## Request permission

For other formats, campaign artwork, co-marketing, white-label use or anything outside the brand kit rules above, contact [legal@null.xyz](mailto:legal@null.xyz) before publishing. Include what you want to use, where it will appear and whether the use is commercial.
