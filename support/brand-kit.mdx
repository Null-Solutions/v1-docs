If you are writing about Null, presenting it or linking to it, use the official artwork below rather than recreating the logo or lifting it from a screenshot. Ownership, trademarks and permitted use are set out on [Brand and copyright](/support/brand-and-copyright).

## Logos

| File | Use it on |
| --- | --- |
| [Null logo, green](/images/Logo.svg) | Sage or white backgrounds. |
| [Null logo, sage](/images/LogoDark.svg) | Green or warm black backgrounds. |
| [Null icon mark](/images/LogoIcon.svg) | Avatars, favicons and other tight spaces. |

The mark only appears in four pairings: green on sage, sage on green, warm black on white and white on warm black. Never place it on terracotta, fjord blue or sand. Keep the artwork's proportions and leave clear space around it.

## Colours

| Colour | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft sage | `#DFE4DA` | Primary. The main background. |
| Green | `#23412E` | Primary. Headings, the logo and solid shapes. |
| Warm black | `#26241D` | Base. Body text and dark backgrounds. |
| White | `#FCFCFC` | Base. Neutral backgrounds. |
| Terracotta | `#97533B` | Accent. |
| Fjord blue | `#618AA1` | Accent. |
| Sand | `#D4B16B` | Accent. |

Lead with sage and green. Accents can add variety, but keep one colour dominant.

## Type

Newsreader is the display typeface, used for the one large heading on a page. Lato is used for everything else.
