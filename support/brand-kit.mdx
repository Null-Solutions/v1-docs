If you are writing about Null, presenting it, or linking to it, use the official artwork below rather than recreating the logo or lifting it from a screenshot. These are the only downloadable brand assets published in these docs.

- [Null logo for light backgrounds](/images/Logo.svg)
- [Null logo for dark backgrounds](/images/LogoDark.svg)
- [Null icon mark](/images/LogoIcon.svg)

Match the file to the background it will sit on. Use the full logo wherever there is room for it, and reserve the icon mark for tight spaces such as avatars and favicons, where the wordmark would shrink into illegibility. Keep the artwork's proportions as supplied, and leave enough clear space around it that it stays readable.

<Note>
Do not imply a partnership, endorsement, or official Null product without written permission.
</Note>

## Usage requests

For other formats, campaign artwork, or co-marketing approval, contact the Null team before publishing. Do not recreate the logo, recolor it, or publish unverified assets as official Null materials.
